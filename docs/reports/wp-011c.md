# WP-011c — Audit-wave fixes: map architecture (Fable 2)

Follow-up to WP-011/WP-011b, addressing the lead's full-room-tour findings on the integrated
build. All changes live in `src/map/**`, `docs/manifest/architecture.json`, `tools/capture-map.js`.
Nothing committed. The integrator checkpoint moves in `layout.js` ('server', 'exec', 'cubes')
were left untouched.

## 1. Garage shutter — hammered foil → satin slatted steel

- **Before**: `artifacts/shots/graybox-rooms--garage.png` (tour set) — the roll door sparkled
  with heavy normal noise from the shared `paintedMetal` peel at world tiling.
- **Change**: new map-domain material `shutterSlat` (`src/map/mapmaterials.js`) — brushed set,
  tint `0x8a939c`, metal 0.35, rough ×1.4 (≈0.5 satin after the map), normalScale 0.2.
  `doors.js#defaultShutterPanel` now consumes it and swaps the slat UVs u↔v so the brushed
  streaking runs **vertical** (rain-streak direction on a roll door) instead of along the slats.
  Consistent with the wp-011b grazing-light finding: painted/brushed sets need ns ≤ 0.2 and
  matte-satin roughness on large planes near lights.
- **After**: `artifacts/shots/map-fix--shutter-slats.png` — clean slatted door, no sparkle;
  slat shadow lines carry the read. Shutter animation unchanged (dynamic mesh untouched;
  `worldUVsApplied` flag keeps the retrofit pass off the swapped UVs).

## 2. Garage overhead duct vs beams

- **Before**: same tour shot — `ductMetal` at metal 0.55 + semi-gloss brushed map rendered as
  bright streaky-white bands under the pendants, splitting the ceiling assembly.
- **Change**: `ductMetal` retuned to tint `0x949ba1`, metal 0.28, rough ×1.5, ns 0.3. First
  pass (`0x848c92`) read too dark from below at distance (silhouette against the white deck);
  final tint sits between the beams and the deck so the assembly reads as one painted family.
- **After**: `artifacts/shots/map-fix--garage-ceiling.png` (looking up: duct/beam/deck family),
  `artifacts/shots/map-rooms--garage.png` (from the floor). Also improves mech/loading/server
  lanes which share the material.

## 3. Acoustic ceiling speckle

- **Before**: `graybox-rooms--lobby.png`, `--cubes.png` — the shared `acoustic` set's fissure
  normals shadow into a heavy dirt-speckle under grazing troffer light; tiles read water-stained
  building-wide.
- **Change**: the ceiling planes are mine (`builder.js` slab pass), so they now consume a new
  map-domain name `acousticCalm` = same `acousticSet` with material normalScale dialed to 0.22
  (albedo/tiling untouched, so the geometric T-grid still registers with the in-texture grooves).
  The stained/missing tile variation in service rooms is separate geometry and still reads.
- **After**: `artifacts/shots/map-fix--ceiling-lobby.png`, `--ceiling-cubes.png` — clean tile
  field, grid + troffers carry the ceiling.
- **Request to Fable 3** (texture is theirs, unedited): consider halving the fissure-pit height
  delta (`px.h -= 0.18` → ≈0.08) or the baked `normalStrength 0.6` in `acousticSet` — every
  consumer at default normalScale gets the dirt read under grazing light.

  (Fable 3 props) carry a heavy sparkle texture at this range.

  (`graybox-rooms--rr-w.png`; ceiling speckle addressed by item 3).
- **stair-b**: still veiled toward white in the tour set. Two-part fix, because light cuts
  alone barely moved it (the blown read is hemi light + fog on featureless near-white drywall,
  not fill intensity):
  - `lightplan.js`: shaft wall packs 12 → 8, `stairTop` room grids 14 → 10.
  - **New geometry** `structures.js#shaftDado` (asset `ARCH-STAIR-DADO`): two-tone shaft paint —
    steel-blue dado bands to door-head height at both floors + a slab-line band, 15 mm cosmetic
    panels on all four shaft walls (serverLiner pattern), door/arch spans skipped with a casing
    margin, no colliders. Hue-forward tint (`shaftPaint` 0x81919f) per the wp-011b finding that
    neutral grays lift to white in bright interiors.
  - After: `artifacts/shots/map-rooms--stair-b.png`, `--stair-a1.png` — the shaft walls carry
    tone and structure (slab line, floor-number sign, red fire door, EXIT all legible); the
    residual soft veil up the shaft is scene fog (12 m+ diagonal), not lighting.
