## 4. Muzzle flash & light

- **Flash sprite roll randomized per shot** via `cosmeticRng` (`rotation: roll` on the
  sprite material) plus ±20 % size jitter — bursts no longer stamp the identical star.
- **Point light single-frame flicker**: light decay/power are now per-effect. Muzzle pops
  use `flashDecay = 34` (gone within one 60 Hz frame) with per-shot intensity jitter
  (`0.8 + rng*0.45`), power 110 so the pop visibly licks nearby geometry. The flashbang
  keeps a slower decay (6) so its glow lingers ~0.6 s.
- **Dark-zone illumination verified**: `flash-illum` scenario darkens the service corridor
  (lights + environment dimmed), stages a trooper 6 m ahead, fires one K5 shot and captures
  the exact flash frame. `flash-illum--dark-before.png` (barely visible enemy) →
  `flash-illum--dark-flash.png` (enemy front-lit by the pop, flash star visible) →
  `flash-illum--dark-after.png` (dark again — light fully decayed).
- **Casing sizes read per family**: brass scales pistol < smg < rifle < sniper, shotgun
  ejects green hulls (`fp-weapons--vanta-s12-fire.png`, `fp-weapons--boreal-k5-fire.png`).

## 6. Character perf (before/after with 14 enemies alive)

Three stacked reductions this package, measured with a Playwright probe that trims the
mission to exactly 14 alive enemies, renders, and reads `window.__qa.perf()`:

1. **Shadow-pass threshold** (wave-1 integration already carried the first part of this
   pass): merges under 300 verts (buckles, straps, chevrons, badges, small weapon parts)
   stopped casting. Measured earlier at 28 staged enemies: character-domain draws
   881 → 543 (−38 %).
2. **World-weapon merge** (`buildWorldWeaponModel`, new in `src/weapons/models.js`):
   enemy-carried and dropped weapons never animate their mag/bolt/pump, so the dynamic
   meshes are folded into the static per-material merge and replaced by `Object3D`
   markers at identical transforms (rig reload-IK targets unchanged). Grip bump-texture
   bucket folds into polymer for world models, and only the largest merged mass casts a
   shadow. Weapon cost: 6 meshes / 2 shadow casters → 3–4 meshes / 1 caster.
3. **Layered-gear shadow cull**: `Bag.build(parent, noCast)` — the torso vest/pouch merge
   and the helmet/cap shell merge sit entirely on top of the already-casting jacket/skull
   masses, so their shadow draws were pure duplication and are now skipped.

Numbers (`window.__qa.perf()`; 14 enemies alive, staged in view at 960×540, plus the 2
hostages):

| metric | before (integrated wave-1 + item 1) | after (items 2+3) |
|---|---|---|
| meshes per trooper (incl. carried weapon) | 26 | 23 |
| shadow casters per trooper | 17 | 14 |
| character-domain meshes (14 enemies + 2 hostages) | 403 | 367 |
| character-domain shadow casters | 258 | 223 |
| scene draw calls (identical staging) | 1192 | 1121 |
| scene triangles | 380,690 | 373,746 |

Relative to the pre-WP-013b wave-1 state (26 meshes, ~25 casters per rig — everything
cast), the per-rig main+shadow contribution went ~51 → 37 draws (−27 %), on top of the
4x wave-1 bucket-merge cut. `perf-check` (lobby, gameplay view, 3 staged enemies +
ambient patrols): 642 draw calls / 302,986 triangles — triangle budget (<350 k) passes;
scene-wide draw overage remains in map/prop domain (flagged in WP-013, unchanged).

Rig visual parity after the merges verified by re-running `world-weapons`, `char-anim`
(reload gesture uses the mag marker — support hand still dips to the magwell) and
`char-hostiles` turntables: `world-weapons--carry-{scout,trooper,heavy}.png`,
`char-anim--reload-gesture.png` — identical presentation, ground shadows still solid.

## 7. Idle life

`CharacterRig.update` gained an idle layer (all damped, `cosmeticRng`-desynced per rig):

- **Guards**: slow weight shift (pelvis x-sway + counter torso roll), two out-of-phase
  look-wander sines (~14 s / 33 s periods) on the head, and a **head-lag counter-motion**
  against AI `scanPhase` yaw turns — the head trails the body turn then re-centers, which
  reads as deliberate looking-around.
- **Hostages (kneel)**: breathing idle kept, plus occasional head glances (wider ±wander)
  and a subtle torso sway coupled to the glance.

Evidence: `idle-life--guard-idle-{0,1,2}.png` (4.2 s apart — head/shoulder pose visibly
different in each) and `idle-life--hostage-idle-{0,1}.png` (6 s apart — head turn + torso
angle change while kneeling). Movement was additionally verified numerically (head world
rotation sampled over time) before the amplitudes were tuned up to read on camera.

## 8. VFX final touches

- **Impact particles inherit surface color**: per-hit value jitter (`0.86 + rng*0.24`)
  multiplies both the dust sprite and chip colors, with extra per-chip variance, so debris
  reads as coming off the shaded surface instead of a flat swatch
  (`vfx-suite--impact-*.png`).
- **Smoke volume edge softness**: hard 2-segment fade replaced by smoothstep bloom-in
  (billows also grow from 50 % scale) and smoothstep dissolve-out with a gentle expansion,
  so spawn/despawn have no popping edges (`vfx-suite--smoke-{early,mid,late}.png`).
- **Flashbang**: world-space burst confirmed as owned scope (bloom sprite + lingering
  point light + sparkle cloud); the screen-space afterimage remains UI's job — no overlap
  added (`vfx-suite--{flashbang,flash-sparkle}.png`).
- **Glass debris**: shards fall under gravity, clamp at the slab under the break
  (floor-level aware for mezzanine breaks), zero their spin on contact, lie flat, and fade
  out — nothing sinks through the floor and nothing persists
  (`vfx-suite--impact-lobby-glass.png`).
- No impact decals added — decals are the props agent's domain per the brief.
