# WP-013 — Characters, weapon art, animation & effects (Fable 4)

Status: complete. Character rebuild, first-person arms + presentation, weapon models pass,
and VFX suite polish are implemented, registered, manifested, and verified through capture
scenarios with zero console errors. All stable interfaces (`CharacterRig`, `VfxSystem`,
`buildWeaponModel` parts contract, `ViewModel`) are unchanged.

## What changed

### Characters — `src/characters/humanoid.js` (rebuilt)

- Proportioned 1.8 m articulated humanoids: pelvis/torso/head/arm(fore,hand)/leg(shin,foot)
  hierarchy with joint spheres and overlapped capsules — no gaps in any pose.
- Layered clothing: jacket blocks over shirt fronts (civilians get open-jacket plackets +
  collar V), belts with buckles, cuffs, boots with toe caps.
- Faces: procedural 128 px canvas (eyes, iris, lids, brows, nose/mouth shading, optional
  stubble, balaclava variant). Reads clearly at 1–3 m. Six hostile head combos
  (skin tone x hair color x hair style x beard/stubble) cycle across spawns — ≥4 distinct
  hostile faces guaranteed.
- Hostile variants: scout (patrol cap + kestrel patch, drop-leg holster, light webbing),
  trooper (helmet with chin straps + rails, plate vest, belly mag pouches, radio pouch),
  heavy (mandible guard + goggles, heavier vest, shoulder pads, drum pouch, wider build).
  All share the dark utility palette (#2e3236 family) + red armband (#8e3b34) with a pale
  chevron motif (original "Kestrel" insignia).
- Hostages: civ0 "D. Okafor" (slate jacket, white shirt, lanyard + badge, dark skin, short
  black hair) and civ1 "M. Lindqvist" (tan jacket, broader build 1.06, thinning gray hair +
  beard). Beard geometry sits proud of the face plane (was occluded; fixed).
- Draw-call control: per-bone geometry merges into four shared vertex-colored bucket
  materials (cloth/gear/skin/metal). A full character is ~4.3–5.8k tris in ~19–30 meshes.
- Animations (procedural, in-rig, all damped):
  - walk/run cycles with arm swing, run lean, weapon-carry pose;
  - aim pose with the weapon shouldered — both hands placed by two-bone IK onto the actual
    `gripMain`/`gripSupport` markers of the attached weapon model (no floating hands);
  - fire recoil pulse (triggered via `getMuzzleWorld`, which the AI calls only when firing);
  - reload gesture (support hand to magazine), flinch with random direction;
  - two death variants — crumple (knees give, torso slumps, tips sideways) and backfall
    (arch, arms out, knee up). Arm rest orientations were solved numerically so limbs settle
    into the ground plane for either tip side (no more skyward arms);
  - kneel + cower with breathing idle for hostages/suppressed enemies;
  - weapon drop on death: the carried model reparents at its world transform and tosses to a
    flat rest beside the body.
- Cold-air breath vapor: single pooled sprite per rig, interval 2.8–4.4 s, exterior envelope
  check, ~1.1 s rise-and-fade puff.

### Weapon models — `src/weapons/models.js` (rebuilt)

All 8 defs rebuilt as original designs with distinct silhouettes (556–1224 tris each,
3-bucket vertex-color merge: metal/polymer/textured-grip; emissive sights/glass keep their
own material). `buildWeaponModel(defId)` still returns `{group, muzzle, mag, bolt, pump}`
plus new `gripMain`/`gripSupport` markers consumed by the rigs and viewmodel.

- karst-p9: slide with serrations + ejection port, recessed barrel, knurled grip, 3-dot sights.
- boreal-k5: side-folder stock profile, vented handguard, curved mag, charging handle.
- halcyon-hc4: full-length rail with teeth, birdcage muzzle device, selector lever, 30-rd mag.
- vanta-s12: tube magazine, ribbed pump sleeve, bead sight, receiver loading port.
- meridian-lr8: long fluted barrel + brake, scope tube with tinted emissive glass, bipod stubs.
- cq-blade: drop-point blade with satin finish, guard, knurled grip, pale spine band.
- fb-3 / sg-2: cylinder devices with vent ring/emitter crown, amber vs teal ID bands, pull rings.
- Iron sights align at `SIGHT_Y = 0.062` (verified in ADS captures).

### First-person presentation — `src/weapons/viewmodel.js`

- FP ARMS: sleeved forearms (tapered cylinder + cuff + amber strap) with gloved mitten hands
  (palm, curled fingers, knuckle plate, thumb). Arms live inside the weapon group and pose
  per-frame: right hand on `gripMain`, support hand on `gripSupport`, chasing the magazine
  during reloads and riding the pump during cycling; hidden for knife/throwables.
- Per-weapon hip placement (lower-right framing, muzzle ~55–65 % screen height), ADS pose
  derived from SIGHT_Y, scope sink handoff to the UI overlay kept.
- Procedural animations improved: draw rise with settle, holster drop, reload roll + mag
  drop + hand chase, pump slide with support hand, throw wind-up/whip, melee diagonal slash.
- Sway/roll fix: lateral velocity now derives from player yaw (the old code read a
  non-existent `rightVec`), landing dip and bob amplitudes retuned.

### VFX — `src/vfx/particles.js` + new `src/vfx/firecontext.js`

- Fire context side-channel: both `getMuzzleWorld` call sites publish the firing weapon's
  family so the VFX system styles effects without any API change.
- Muzzle flash variants: pistol cross-flash (small), SMG/rifle star, shotgun wide bloom,
  sniper long flash; forward spike on long guns; brief barrel smoke wisp; light pulse.
- Impacts per material: concrete chips + dust, drywall powder puff, wood splinters, metal
  sparks + ring flash, glass glitter, carpet fluff, snow puff. Counts scale with the
  renderer quality scalar.
- Tracers thinned (opacity 0.6); casings sized per family with green shotgun hulls.
- Smoke volume: 14 lit billow sprites (bright tops, shaded core), 1.5 s bloom, ~16 s hold
  with a slow pulse, `isSmoked` LOS contract kept.
- Flash burst: white bloom + point light + lingering twinkling sparkle cloud (~1.2 s).
- Blood: restrained dark puffs + pooled small floor decals (max 12, oldest recycled);
  everything suppressed by `reducedBlood`.
- Ambient: soft round snowfall (puff texture — no square flakes), sunbeam dust motes
  (cheap Points) in the atrium/lounge daylight zones.
- All effects pooled through a single spawn list; zero allocations at steady state beyond
  sprite spawn/despawn.

## Known issues

- Scene draw calls (895) exceed the 450 budget scene-wide; the overage lives in map/prop
  geometry outside this package. Flagging for the lead — characters/weapons/VFX are within
  their share (~125).
- The hostage captive shots in the server room are washed out by that room's very bright
  lighting (another agent's lightplan); the same rigs read correctly in neutral light
  (see `_civ-*.png` probes).
- Hostile heads under helmets read best at ≤5 m; at 8 m the face plane resolves to a skin
  block (acceptable at the stylized bar).
- Breath vapor is subtle by design; the capture forces the interval timer to catch it
  mid-puff.
- The dust motes are only visible against the atrium daylight shafts — the capture angle
  shows them faintly.
