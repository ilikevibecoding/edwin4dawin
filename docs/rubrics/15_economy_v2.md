# Rubric 15 — Economy v2: goods chains, inventories, atomic transfers, the price rule, an honest ledger, physical shipments

Source: `docs/overhaul/SPEC.md` §10 (the economy), the economy parts of §9 (port cycle), §15 (surprises that hang on
shipments) and §19 (acceptance: "track a physical shipment from docking to shelf"). Pass 1 (rubric 08) gave the city a
wallet, vendors with daily stock, jobs, housing and a ship dealer; this pass makes the numbers real: every business has
an inventory that comes from somewhere, every credit and every unit of stock is accounted for, and a shipment is a
thing in the world you can follow from a freighter's hold to a shelf.

Everything below is measurable by `scripts/test-economy.mjs` (offline + `--url` CDP) and `scripts/sim-economy.mjs`
(multi-day headless run). "Business" means a `Business` record of `game.economy` (one per Coruscant lot whose purpose
trades, produces or serves); "good" means a key of `GOODS` in `src/economy/prices.js`.

## Acceptance criteria

| # | Criterion | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Goods catalogue.** `GOODS` carries the ten wholesale categories of §10 as bulk goods (`staples`, `water`, `fuel`, `parts`, `components`, `medical`, `textiles`, `salvage`, `waste`, plus prepared food as the retail food items) with a base price and a category each, and every retail item a purpose kind sells maps to exactly one bulk input (`bulkOf(key)`); the whole catalogue stays under 80 keys and the wholesale part under 12. Each good is documented (base price, category, who supplies / consumes it) in `docs/overhaul/economy.md`. | offline: catalogue shape, `bulkOf` total, doc table matches the code |
| 2 | **Businesses.** Every lot whose purpose sells, buys, consumes, produces, supplies or serves is a `Business` `{ id, name, kind, category, district, stock: Map, target: Map, capacity, reserved: Map, funds, hours, serviceCapability, suppliers, customers, reorderRule, open(hour) }`, derived deterministically from `purposeFor(lot)` and the layout: the nearest wholesale node (customs terminal, depot, warehouse) is the supplier of a retail / service business, wholesale nodes are supplied by the cargo terminal, the terminal by offworld freighters; residential lots are the customers of the nearest food / utility / clinic businesses. `game.economy.business(lotId)` returns the record; two fresh economies of the same layout produce identical businesses. | offline: >= 250 businesses, every supplier id resolves, determinism (JSON equality) |
| 3 | **Household demand in batches.** Each residential lot has one household record `{ lotId, size, budget }`; demand is a daily meal batch (`size` meals), a daily utility batch, a domestic batch every few days and `size × 0.1` treatments a day, bought at the nearest matching businesses during their opening hours — never per meal per person. Household purchases are journaled as `household` sources and the consumed goods as `consumption` sinks. | offline sim: meals bought per day within ±25% of Σ size; no per-person entries |
| 4 | **The five chains are linked by shipments, not teleports.** (a) staples: offworld → cargo pad → terminal → depot/warehouse → diner/market → residents; (b) parts: terminal → depot → workshop (`repair_shop`/`droid_shop`/`hangar`) → consumed by repairs → `repairBerths()` availability; (c) medical: terminal → clinic/pharmacy/bacta ward → patients (bacta shots need stock); (d) salvage: waste → recycling plant (`waste → salvage`) → foundry / droid shop (`salvage → parts`) → maintenance; (e) public funds → utility (`power_plant`, `refinery`) and transit → `serviceLevel`. Every link is a `Shipment` record that spends time in `in_transit`; stock never appears at the receiver without a preceding `unloaded/delivered` transition. | offline 7-day sim: every chain has >= 1 delivered shipment and the downstream consumption > 0; no receiver stock change without a shipment |
| 5 | **Atomic transfers.** `transfer({ from, to, good, qty, credits, reason, key })` checks stock (minus reserved), funds, receiver capacity and permission, then updates both sides exactly once and appends one journal entry; it returns `true` or a reason string (`'no-stock'`, `'reserved'`, `'no-funds'`, `'no-capacity'`, `'not-permitted'`, `'bad-request'`) and never leaves a partial change. A transfer with an already-journaled `key` returns `true` without applying again. | offline: each failure reason leaves both accounts byte-identical; success changes exactly the four fields; same key twice = one change |
| 6 | **No duplication under interruption.** A double click, an interrupted UI, a save/reload mid-transaction and two simultaneous customers cannot duplicate items or credits: the player's buy path is guarded by an intent id, the journal of applied keys is persisted and restored, and queued customers take turns on the last unit. | offline: two customers for the last unit -> one success + `'no-stock'`; serialize/restore between the debit and the credit is impossible (single synchronous step) and re-applying the persisted key is a no-op; CDP: buy, `persistNow()`, reload -> stock and wallet match exactly |
| 7 | **Player and jobs go through transfers.** `buy`, `sell`, `rent`, rides, bacta shots, ship purchases and job payouts each produce journal entries (`economy:transfer` events); a job payout is keyed by the job id so it can never pay twice. Creative mode still charges nothing but logs the goods as a `creative` sink. | CDP: `game.events.recent('economy:transfer')` grows by the expected entries; offline: payout key replay is a no-op |
| 8 | **Price rule.** `quote(lotId, good)` -> `{ buy, sell, stock, factor, disruption }` with `buy = round(base × district × (clamp(target / available, 0.75, 1.75) + disruption))`, `disruption ∈ [-0.25, +0.35]` (documented components: overdue orders, detained shipments, oversupply), `sell = round(buy(after the sale) × 0.45)` or `null` when the business does not trade the category, has no funds or no room. Empty shelves quote the ceiling. | offline: factor bounds over a sweep, monotonic in stock, disruption bounded, empty = 1.75 |
| 9 | **Arbitrage converges.** Buying low at one business and selling high at another, unit by unit, has a non-increasing margin that reaches <= 0 within the two businesses' stock/funds limits; total credits over (player + A + B) are constant through the loop; a second loop after convergence earns nothing. | offline: loop test on two real businesses |
| 10 | **Honest ledger.** Every stock or credit change is a journal entry. `ledger.sources` (imports, allocations, household spending, fares & port fees, purchases from the player, production value added, admin grants) and `ledger.sinks` (consumption, wages, maintenance, disposal, exports, import payments, retail to the player, levies, creative takes). Over a multi-day run `Σ sources − Σ sinks == W_end − W_start` exactly, where `W = Σ business funds + Σ stock × base + player credits + Σ shipment cargo × base`. Negative stock never occurs. | sim 7 days: identity holds to the credit; negative-stock count 0 |
| 11 | **Imports are bound to real freighter arrivals.** An import shipment is `ordered` until a real cargo-pad freighter of W2's traffic leaves on its loop; at that moment it is `loaded` (the import source entry) and rides the hold (`in_transit`); `arrived` at touchdown; `unloaded` while the doors are open onto the pad-side stack; a conveyor leg carries it to the terminal (`delivered`). Nothing arrives without a landing: with the traffic absent, no import is ever loaded. | CDP: watch the freighter of a cargo pad through one cycle and assert the transitions in order with the ship's phase; offline: arrivals provider returning none -> imports stay `ordered` |
| 12 | **Physical shipments are visible.** `shipments()` returns live records `{ id, goods, qty, from, to, state, carrier: { kind: 'ship' \| 'courier' \| 'conveyor', id }, position: { x, y, z }, eta }`. Instanced crate stacks (<= 4 draw calls total) stand at the cargo-terminal docks and the loading bays of depots/warehouses sized by their bulk stock; a freighter carrying a shipment shows crates in its hold (drawn from the economy's side at the ship's pose); the hold crates vanish when unloaded. `detain(id, reason)` / `release(id)` exist for later builders and `economy:shipment` fires on every state change. | CDP: crate instance count > 0 at a stack, hold crates present in `in_transit`/`arrived` and gone after `unloaded`; draw-call delta of the crate layer <= 4 |
| 13 | **Visible economic state.** The shop shows real stock counts and both quotes; `menuFor(business)` changes when a diner's stock runs low (items go "off the menu"); `waitingFor(lotId)` names the good and shipment a mechanic waits for; `holdFor(shipId)` reports a freighter held for unpaid cargo; `noticeFor(district)` reports a delayed route, a price spike or a shortage; the admin panel "Economy" section lists ledger totals, businesses by funds/stock, shipments in transit with states, quotes of 5 goods at 3 businesses and the day counter. | CDP: drain a diner and diff `menuFor().text`; panel section exists and refreshes |
| 14 | **Stabilisers are explicit and logged.** Essential services (clinic, pharmacy, bacta ward, power plant, refinery, transit) that cannot pay for their input receive a logged public allocation (`allocation` source) bounded per day; a terminal that cannot pay an import bill holds the cargo (the ship's `holdFor` is set) instead of spawning credits. After a shortage, service uptime recovers within the run. | sim: essential-service uptime >= 90% over 7 days with the scripted disruptions; every allocation appears in the ledger |
| 15 | **Balance.** Basic meal 6-16 cr (bread 8 / cooked meat 12-16 at factor 0.75-1.0), local ride 5-15 (12-15 base), a useful small component ~25 (`parts` 25, `door` 25, `holo_sign` 24), a short delivery 60-150 (W5's delivery jobs pay cost + 40% on 60-240 cr of goods; courier runs 30-120). W5's jobs and ship dealer still work through the new transfer path. | offline: book values; CDP: courier + ship purchase still pass |
| 16 | **Multi-day simulation.** `node scripts/sim-economy.mjs --days 7` runs the real layout/purposes with the real freighter schedule (no rendering), injects a route disruption, a detained medical shipment and a salvage shortfall, and reports conservation, negative-stock count (0), price bounds, essential-service uptime, shipments delivered %, and a per-day summary; the run finishes in under a minute. | run it |
| 17 | **Save.** Businesses, households, shipments, the ledger totals and the applied-key journal persist under the `economy` save key with defaults, so a W5 save (wallet only) loads and gets a fresh v2 state; a reload mid-day reproduces stock, funds and in-flight shipments. | offline: serialize/restore equality; CDP: reload keeps a drained shelf drained |
| 18 | **Budget and switch.** `?economy=0` disables the v2 simulation and crates (W5 wallet only). With it on, at the Senate view (`x=2975 z=120 y=97.2 yaw=0 pitch=-2 quality=light rd=10`): <= +4 ms JS/frame, <= +20 draw calls, <= +40 MB heap vs `economy=0`; offscreen businesses are simulated in coarse batches on a tick timer, never per frame. | `scripts/bench.mjs` before/after |
| 19 | **Determinism.** No `Math.random`: business seeds come from lot seeds, daily demand from `hash2(lot.seed, day)`, the journal from the game clock; two runs of the sim with the same seed produce identical journals. | sim twice, compare |
| 20 | **Tests stay green.** `npm run test-unit`, `npm test`, `node scripts/test-economy.mjs` (offline) and `--url` all pass. | run them |
