# Rubric 17 — The Galactic Senate as a true centrepiece

Goal (SPEC §8, plus the Senate rows of §7, §13 and §15): the Senate stops being a grey dome with a stepped bowl inside
and becomes the civic heart of the city — a monumental chamber you can walk into and up through, twelve delegations
whose offices you can enter and tell apart, sessions that happen on a clock, three policy decisions whose outcomes
other systems can hear, Jedi with a reason to be there, and two routes (visitor and service) that really work.

Owner: P4. Files: `src/coruscant/landmarks/senate.js`, `src/senate/**`, `src/ui/senate.js/.css`,
`scripts/test-senate.mjs`, `docs/overhaul/senate.md`. Test: `node scripts/test-senate.mjs [--url http://localhost:PORT]`.

## Design notes

- The chamber is a vertical drum: a stepped lower bowl gives scale at the floor, three wall tiers of cantilevered pods
  with a gallery ring behind each give the "wall of platforms" of the Databank description, the public gallery on top
  gives the reveal. Distant pods are ordinary blocks (the same geometry as the playable ones, no fake counting).
- Suites live in the drum's outer band at the two lower wall tiers (six per tier), between the pod galleries and the
  outer corridor, so the pod door is private (back corridor → pod) and the front door is public (corridor → reception).
  The delegation lifts rise from the grand lobby ring, one per suite, so the lobby shows twelve lift doors with plaques.
- Sessions are pure functions of `(seed, game hour)`; the runtime class only samples `game.sky.time` and emits the
  transitions it crosses, so a player who leaves and returns finds the state the clock says.
