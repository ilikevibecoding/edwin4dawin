## 4. What the scan does and does not mean

The falsification scan is the strongest independent evidence to date that the
framework's *target* inequality is true: it survives every known
non-log-concave tree (where the stronger log-concavity route dies) and every
forest tested. It is evidence, not proof. Two structural facts fall out and
are worth carrying into the proof effort:

1. At a descent index, log-concavity at `r` implies `ISO_r`; so a
   descent-conditional violation needs an LC break at a descent index
   `r < L(α)`. Every LC break observed (n ≤ 29, Galvin, PatternBoost) sits at
   `k ≥ α−8 > L(α)`.
2. At an ascent index, an LC break at `r` forces `ISO_r < 0`; so the
   unconditional target theorem implies "no forest breaks log-concavity before
   its mode". The star `K_{1,n−1}` is the extremal object at every `n`
   (`Q_2` slack ≈ `12/n²` → 0): the target inequality is asymptotically tight
   on stars, and any universal proof must handle stars exactly.
