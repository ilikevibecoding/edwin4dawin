## 2. Literal forest form

Every candidate is a product of explicitly stored rooted tree components
`(R_i,s_i)`.  The search computes, using integer polynomial arithmetic,

```text
B = product I(R_i),
C = product I(R_i-s_i),
P = (1+x)B+xC.
```

Adding a new vertex `p` adjacent to every marked root `s_i`, and then adding
a leaf `ell` adjacent to `p`, constructs a tree with independence polynomial
`P`.  Thus all comparisons are made in the component-separated forest class;
the search does not use generic PF rows.

Each component polynomial is generated by the rooted include/exclude
recurrence

```text
E_v = product (E_u+S_u),
S_v = x product E_u,
I(R_v)=E_v+S_v.
```

PGC is checked in its full required prefix by the exact cleared integer
margin

```text
k b_(k-2) G_k(P) - (k-1) p_(k-1) G_(k-1)(B).
```

When `beta=deg(B)=2 mod 3` and `deg(B+xC)=beta`, the search also checks the
surviving coupled boundary expression

```text
D_(r+1)(B)+D_r(B)+D_r(C),
D_j(Q)=3q_j-q_(j-1),  r=floor(2 beta/3).
```

## 6. Evidence boundary

The explicit parameter rectangle is exhaustive only within the displayed
family and bounds.  The mutation and beam lanes are deterministic bounded
heuristics; they do not cover all rooted trees, all component multisets, or
all forests of any large order.  The absence of a witness therefore does not
prove PGC, Boundary-SM3, or unimodality.  It narrows this particular literal
adversarial lane and leaves the all-order coupled inequalities open.
