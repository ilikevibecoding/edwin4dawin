# Literature status refresh for Erdős Problem 993

Date: 2026-08-29

## Outcome

The public record still lists the Alavi--Malde--Schwenk--Erdős tree/forest
independence-sequence conjecture as **open**.  I found no public proof and no
finite counterexample in the sources checked below.  This refresh is a status
check only; it supplies no mathematical input to the local proof program.

## Current primary/public sources checked

1. The Erdős Problems entry for #993 labels the problem `FALSIFIABLE Open`,
   lists no partial or complete solution in its incorporated remarks, and was
   last edited on 2026-02-01:

   <https://www.erdosproblems.com/993>

2. Reynolds' March 2026 frozen manuscript reports exact exhaustive
   verification through all 8,691,747,673 unlabeled trees of order at most 29,
   but explicitly states that the general conjecture remains open:

   <https://zenodo.org/records/19100781>

3. Li's March 2026 preprint proves unimodality for the two known infinite
   non-log-concave families `T_{3,m,n}` and `T^*_{3,m,n}`.  It states the full
   forest conjecture as Conjecture 1.1 rather than claiming a resolution:

   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03025>

4. Hibi--Kara--Vien's April 2026 preprint studies constructions with symmetric
   unimodal independence polynomials and explicitly says that the conjecture
   remains open in general:

   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18824>

5. The 2025 rooted-product paper proves real-rootedness, hence unimodality, for
   a restricted rooted-product class and describes the general conjecture as
   unresolved:

   <https://mta.csu.edu.cn/CN/10.3969/j.issn.1006-8074.2025.02.004>

## Scope guard

The exhaustive order-29 verification and the infinite-family theorems are
strong evidence and useful boundary checks, but neither can be promoted to an
all-order proof.  Conversely, absence of a published counterexample is not a
proof.  Any local resolution must still be an independently replayable
all-order proof or a finite exact counterexample.
