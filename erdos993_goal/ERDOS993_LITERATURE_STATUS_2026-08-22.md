# Erdős Problem #993 — literature status checked 2026-08-22

## Bottom line

I found no published proof or finite counterexample resolving the statement that the independent-set sequence of every tree (equivalently, the tree case underlying the forest question) is unimodal. The sources below still present the general conjecture as open. This is a bounded literature check, not proof that no unindexed or private result exists.

## Current evidence

- The Erdős Problems entry labels #993 **Open** and **Falsifiable**, reports no claimed partial or complete solution in its comments, and was last edited 2026-02-01: <https://www.erdosproblems.com/993>
- Reynolds' current 2026 project manuscript reports exhaustive verification through all trees of order at most 29 and explicitly says the conjecture remains open: <https://zenodo.org/records/19100781>
- Li's 2026 paper proves unimodality for the two known infinite families of non-log-concave tree independence polynomials, not for arbitrary trees: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03025>
- Yosef, Mizrachi, and Kadrawi's 2021 paper treats the conjecture as open and proves/checks restricted cases: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.06744>
- Basit and Galvin's work gives asymptotic/random-tree and structural partial results rather than a universal theorem: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.12562>
- Heilman's random-tree work likewise concerns probabilistic/typical behavior rather than all trees: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04756>

## Claim discipline for this project

Until every symbolic sector, boundary face, redistribution bridge, and forest-composition step has a checked exact certificate, the present work must be described as a partial computer-assisted proof program. Finite scans and sector certificates are evidence for their stated domains only; they are not a proof of the all-tree conjecture by themselves.
