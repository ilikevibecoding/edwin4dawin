# Erdős Problem 993 literature-status refresh

Date checked: 2026-08-17

Status: **no prior complete proof or finite counterexample located; the public
problem remains OPEN.**

The current Erdős Problems entry still labels #993 “FALSIFIABLE Open” and
states that no partial or complete solution is claimed in its comments:

```text
https://www.erdosproblems.com/993
https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/993?order=oldest
```

The newest substantive public comment located reports exhaustive unimodality
through 29 vertices and explicitly says that its structural closure route is
still conditional; it does not claim a proof.  The associated public
repository is

```text
https://github.com/BrettRey/erdos-problem-993
```

Targeted 2026 arXiv searches for “independence polynomial”, “independent set
sequence”, and “tree unimodality” found family/existence results and finite
support, but no universal resolution.  In particular,

```text
https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18824
```

studies the existence of symmetric and unimodal independence polynomials of
trees; its abstract does not claim that every tree has a unimodal sequence.
The older direct paper

```text
https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.06744
```

provides finite computational support, not an all-tree proof.

This refresh supplements the broader primary-source audit in
`FINAL_ROUTE_LITERATURE_AND_SCHUR_LIFT_AUDIT_2026-08-13.md`.  Absence from a
targeted search is not a mathematical theorem, so a final claimed resolution
must still be compared against the live literature once the proof or
counterexample itself is complete.
