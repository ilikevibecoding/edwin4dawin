# Erdos Problem #993 - literature status checked 2026-08-27

## Bottom line

The public problem remains open. This targeted refresh found no published
universal proof and no finite non-unimodal tree or forest. It is not proof
that no private, unindexed, or newly submitted result exists, so the search
must be repeated before any public claim of resolution.

## Current status evidence

- The current Erdos Problems entry labels #993 **Open** and **Falsifiable**,
  and says that no complete or partial solution is claimed in its comments:
  <https://www.erdosproblems.com/993>
- The public discussion records exhaustive tree checks through order 29 and
  further searches among non-log-concave trees, while explicitly declining
  to claim a full solution:
  <https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/993>
- Grace M. X. Li, *Unimodality of independence polynomials of two family of
  trees*, proves unimodality for two particular infinite families of
  non-log-concave trees, not for every tree:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03025>
- Takayuki Hibi, Selvi Kara, and Dalena Vien, *Symmetric and unimodal
  independence polynomials of trees*, studies existence constructions, not
  the universal conjecture:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18824>
- Alina Du, Steven Heilman, and Greta Panova, arXiv:2605.02193, concerns
  dominating-set sequences rather than a resolution of the independent-set
  conjecture:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02193>

## Search scope and claim discipline

On 2026-08-27, targeted web and arXiv searches covered the exact problem
number, the conjecture statement, the phrases `independent set sequence` and
`independence polynomial` with trees and forests, and recent 2026 results.
No located source claimed either a universal proof or a finite counterexample.

The local certificate program therefore remains new, unpublished partial
work until every rank and composition gate is closed and independently
replayed. A rank-eight theorem, a finite census, or an all-rank boundary face
cannot by itself be promoted to a solution of Problem #993.
