# Erdos Problem 993 literature refresh

Date checked: 2026-08-25

Targeted searches of the current Erdős Problems entry and arXiv found no
claimed complete proof and no finite counterexample. The problem entry still
labels #993 open and reports no complete or partial solution in its comments:

- https://www.erdosproblems.com/993

The most directly relevant recent primary papers remain partial:

- Grace M. X. Li, *Unimodality of independence polynomials of two family of
  trees*, arXiv:2603.03025. It proves unimodality for the two known infinite
  Kadrawi--Levit non-log-concave families, not for all trees:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03025
- Takayuki Hibi, Selvi Kara, and Dalena Vien, *Symmetric and unimodal
  independence polynomials of trees*, arXiv:2604.18824. It studies existence
  constructions with symmetric unimodal independence polynomials, not the
  universal conjecture: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18824
- Eric Ramos and Sunny Sun, *An AI enhanced approach to the tree unimodality
  conjecture*, arXiv:2510.18826. It reports many additional counterexamples to
  log-concavity, but no counterexample to unimodality:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18826

Searches explicitly targeting May--August 2026 arXiv identifiers returned no
newer universal resolution. This establishes only the state of the targeted
search as of the date above; it is not a proof that no unindexed manuscript
exists.
