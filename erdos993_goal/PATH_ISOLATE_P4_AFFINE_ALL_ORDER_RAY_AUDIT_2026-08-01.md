# Stable path P4: affine all-order ray audit

This is an exact finite audit of the remaining positive-intersection
affine bridge on a structured family of parameter points. It is evidence
for the uniform theorem, not a proof for all parameters.

## Audited parameter set

At \(x=0\), the audit uses the union of

\[
\{(c,m):c=1,\ 3\le m\le20\},
\]

\[
\{(c,m):m=3,\ 1\le c\le20\},
\]

and the three balanced rays

\[
c=\max\{1,\lfloor m/2\rfloor+\delta\},
\qquad
3\le m\le20,
\qquad
\delta\in\{-1,0,1\}.
\]

After duplicates are removed this gives 86 parameter triples and 172
parity cases.

## Why the audit covers every order

For each point and parity, let

\[
F_r=A^aS^bW^r(B^\vee_\epsilon+rP^\vee)
\]

and let \((N,N)\) be the fixed reciprocal target. The computation first
checks the desired exact integer coefficient \([z^Nw^N]F_r\) at every
order before cone entry. It then finds the first \(r_0\) for which every
coefficient of \(F_{r_0}\) in

\[
0\le i,j\le N
\]

is nonnegative. The propagation identity

\[
F_{r+1}=WF_r+A^aS^bW^{r+1}P^\vee
\]

then proves the square, and hence the desired coefficient, nonnegative at
every \(r\ge r_0\).

Thus the computation is exhaustive in Newton order for every sampled
parameter point; it is not merely a bounded-order stress test.

## Result

All 172 parity cases pass for all orders. There are no negative
pre-entry central coefficients. Every case enters the southwest-square
cone, and the maximum entry order is 28.

The entry pattern is piecewise-linear on the extreme rays. In the tested
range the far \(m=3\) ray stabilizes at

\[
r_0=2c-12
\]

for \(c\ge15\), in both parities. The \(c=1\) ray also becomes linear in
\(m\), with a one-order parity offset. Balanced rays enter immediately
or after only a few orders.

This points to a finite cone partition: prove square entry separately on
the two imbalanced rays, then cover the balanced middle cone with a much
smaller entry bound.

## Ruled-out strengthening

The entry polynomial is not globally coefficientwise nonnegative. A
14-case representative audit found negative coefficients outside the
relevant square in every case. Likewise the raw parameter-ray correction
kernels \(\Delta_mB+P\) and \(\Delta_cB+2P\) remain signed. Therefore the
cutoff at \(0\le i,j\le N\) is essential; a successful induction must
include its moving boundary band.
