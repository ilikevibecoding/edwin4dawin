# Stable path P4: sixth support diagonal

For the general two-layer lift residual
\[
D_\epsilon(c,m,s,x)=
G_\epsilon(c,m+1,s,x)-G_\epsilon(c,m,s,x),
\]
the inequality
\[
D_\epsilon(c,m,4,x)\ge0
\]
holds for all \(c,m,x\ge0\) and both parities.

## Polynomial architecture

At \(s=4\), centered support leaves the fixed windows
\[
-8\le v\le7\quad(\epsilon=0),\qquad
-7\le v\le7\quad(\epsilon=1).
\]
After setting \(u=m+v\), every residual path rank in a path-moment
factor is a fixed integer.  Hence each falling-factorial path formula
is an ordinary polynomial in \(c,m,x\).  The only denominators come
from
\[
\frac{\binom{2m+\epsilon}{m+v}}
     {\binom{2m+\epsilon}{m}}
\]
and the adjacent-\(m\) central-binomial ratio.  Consequently,
\[
\begin{aligned}
P_0(c,m,x)
&=\left(\prod_{i=1}^{9}(m+i)\right)
  \frac{D_0(c,m,4,x)}{\binom{2m}{m}},\\
P_1(c,m,x)
&=\left(\prod_{i=2}^{9}(m+i)\right)
  \frac{D_1(c,m,4,x)}{\binom{2m+1}{m}}
\end{aligned}
\]
are polynomials, with coordinatewise degree bounds
\[
\deg(P_0)\le(12,19,10),\qquad
\deg(P_1)\le(11,17,9)
\]
in \((c,m,x)\).  These bounds follow termwise from the fixed support
windows and the four path-moment falling-factorial formulas.

## Exact Newton certificate

Expand each polynomial in the tensor Newton basis:
\[
P_\epsilon(c,m,x)=
\sum_{i,j,k}a^{(\epsilon)}_{ijk}
\binom ci\binom mj\binom xk.
\]
Exact interpolation on the full degree-determining grids gives:

| parity | grid points | nonzero \(a_{ijk}\) | negative \(a_{ijk}\) | smallest nonzero |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 3,528 | 1,124 | 0 | 590,768,640 |
| 1 | 2,717 | 857 | 0 | 247,726,080 |

One additional grid layer in every coordinate gives zero coefficients
beyond each stated degree bound.  Since every Newton basis element is
nonnegative on nonnegative integers, \(P_\epsilon\ge0\), and the
denominator products and central binomial coefficients are positive.
This proves the sixth support diagonal.
