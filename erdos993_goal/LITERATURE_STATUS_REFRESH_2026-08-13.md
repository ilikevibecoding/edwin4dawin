# Literature status refresh — 2026-08-13

This is a current-status check for the Alavi--Malde--Schwenk--Erdos
tree/forest independent-set-sequence conjecture.  It is not part of the
mathematical proof.

## Current verdict

The literature located in this refresh still treats the general conjecture as
open.  In particular:

1. Hibi--Kara--Vien, *Symmetric and unimodal independence polynomials of
   trees* (arXiv:2604.18824), explicitly says that as of April 2026 the
   conjecture remains open in general and records exhaustive verification
   through 29 vertices.
2. Li, *Unimodality of independence polynomials of two family of trees*
   (arXiv:2603.03025), proves unimodality only for the two known infinite
   non-log-concave tree families `T_(3,m,n)` and `T*_(3,m,n)`; its introduction
   still presents the general statement as a conjecture.
3. Heilman, *Independent sets of random trees and of sparse random graphs*,
   explicitly calls the problem open and proves high-probability initial and
   terminal monotonicity results for random trees rather than a deterministic
   all-tree theorem.
4. Basit--Galvin, *On the independent set sequence of a tree* (2021), records
   the same open question and the known terminal-decrease theorem.
5. The Erdős Problems #993 entry continues to list the statement as the
   problem, without a resolved status or a proof/counterexample citation.

The search also found 2026 papers on special families and on constructing
symmetric unimodal tree independence polynomials, but no claimed proof for
all trees/forests and no finite counterexample.

## Caution

Search-index recency and metadata are not a substitute for a proof audit.
This note establishes only that no prior general resolution was located in
the checked current sources.  Before releasing a claimed resolution, repeat
the search and have the final argument independently checked.
