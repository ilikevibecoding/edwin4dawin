# Literature status refresh for Erdős Problem #993

Date: 2026-08-16

## Verdict

The Alavi--Malde--Schwenk--Erdős conjecture remains publicly **open**.  I
found no paper, preprint, repository, or official problem-page update that
claims a proof for all trees/forests or gives a finite non-unimodal tree.

## Current primary sources checked

1. Erdős Problems #993:
   <https://www.erdosproblems.com/993>

   The page still labels the problem `FALSIFIABLE Open`.  Its current public
   discussion records exhaustive verification through 29 vertices but no
   claimed solution.

2. Brett Reynolds' current computational/manuscript repository:
   <https://github.com/BrettRey/erdos-problem-993>

   The README explicitly says, as of August 2026, that the repository does
   not contain a proof.  It reports exact exhaustive unimodality checks for
   all 8,691,747,673 trees through order 29 and lists several conditional or
   family-specific structural routes.

3. Grace M. X. Li, *Unimodality of independence polynomials of two family of
   trees*, arXiv:2603.03025:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03025>

   This proves unimodality only for the two known infinite families whose
   independence polynomials need not be log-concave.

4. Takayuki Hibi, Selvi Kara, and Dalena Vien, *Symmetric and unimodal
   independence polynomials of trees*, arXiv:2604.18824:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18824>

   This studies existence/classification of symmetric unimodal examples, not
   the universal conjecture.

5. Exact August 2026 arXiv/web searches for the phrases `independence
   polynomial`, `independent set sequence`, `tree unimodality`, and `Erdős
   Problem 993` produced no newer universal proof or counterexample.

## Consequence for this workspace

The current local rank-seven `V_7` and boundary theorems are new proof-route
artifacts, not confirmation of a known published solution.  The goal must
remain open until the all-forest `Q_7` reserve and then an all-rank bridge are
proved, or until a finite tree/forest counterexample is independently
verified.
