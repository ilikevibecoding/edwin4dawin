# Erdős Problem #993 — literature status checked 2026-08-25

## Bottom line

The public problem remains open.  This refresh found no published universal
proof and no finite non-unimodal tree or forest.  This is a targeted search,
not proof that no unindexed, private, or newly submitted result exists.

## Current status evidence

- The current Erdős Problems entry labels #993 **Open** and **Falsifiable**
  and lists no claimed complete or partial solution:
  <https://www.erdosproblems.com/993>
- Its public discussion records exhaustive checks through order 29 and later
  searches among non-log-concave trees, but explicitly does not claim a full
  solution: <https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/993?embed=1>
- A July 28, 2026 SciNet computational finding reports exact verification of
  all forests on at most 30 vertices and a closure theorem for forests whose
  components all have order at most 30.  It explicitly leaves trees of order
  at least 31, and hence the universal conjecture, open.  The finding links a
  fixed GitHub commit, run logs, and an independent reproduction, but it is
  not a universal proof or a finite counterexample:
  <https://api.scinet.pub/f/25b8f9a4-efab-4707-9b77-18d3702ecbb3>
- Grace M. X. Li's March 2026 preprint proves unimodality for two particular
  infinite families of non-log-concave trees, not for all trees:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03025>
- Hibi, Kara, and Vien's April 2026 preprint studies the existence of symmetric
  unimodal tree independence polynomials, not the universal conjecture:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18824>
- Yosef, Mizrachi, and Kadrawi's earlier paper supplies restricted and finite
  computational support while stating the universal conjecture:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.06744>

## Fresh-search scope

On 2026-08-25, targeted searches covered the exact problem number, the phrases
`independence polynomial of trees` and `tree unimodality conjecture`, arXiv
submissions for May through August 2026, and the July 2026 SciNet forest
finding above.  The newest directly relevant conventional preprints located
were the March and April 2026 restricted-family/existence results.  No source
found in this refresh claimed either a universal proof or a finite
counterexample.
