# Path–isolate P4: Newton-quotient finite-base reduction

> **Superseded target.**  The underlying general two-layer lift is
> false.  Its first exact failure is
> \((c,m,s,x,\epsilon)=(0,8,6,45,0)\).  The quotient identities and
> low-order coefficient theorems in this note remain valid, but the
> all-order positivity claim does not.  See
> `PATH_ISOLATE_P4_GENERAL_LAYER_LIFT_COUNTEREXAMPLE_2026-07-30.md`.

For the general two-layer lift residual
\[
D_\epsilon(c,m,s,x)=
G(c,m+1,s,x,\epsilon)-G(c,m,s,x,\epsilon),
\]
form its Newton polynomial at \(s=-1\):
\[
F_\epsilon(c,m,x;z)=
\sum_{r\ge0}\Delta_s^rD_\epsilon(c,m,-1,x)z^r.
\]

Exact calculation supports the factorization
\[
F_\epsilon(c,m,x;z)=
(1+z)^{2c+2m+x-1}P_\epsilon(c,m,x;z).
\tag{1}
\]
Every coefficient of \(P_\epsilon\) is nonnegative in all tested
cases.  Moreover, the data support the three coefficientwise
recurrences
\[
\begin{aligned}
P_\epsilon(c+1,m,x;z)&\succeq P_\epsilon(c,m,x;z),\\
P_\epsilon(c,m+1,x;z)&\succeq P_\epsilon(c,m,x;z),\\
P_\epsilon(c,m,x+1;z)&\succeq P_\epsilon(c,m,x;z).
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]

## Conditional finite-base theorem

Assume (1) and (2) for \(c,m,x\ge0\), \(c+m\ge4\), and
\(\epsilon\in\{0,1\}\).  Then the general two-layer lift holds for
all these parameters.

Indeed, any pair \((c,m)\) with \(c+m\ge4\) dominates
coordinatewise at least one pair
\[
(0,4),(1,3),(2,2),(3,1),(4,0).
\]
Repeated use of (2), followed by the \(x\)-recurrence, reduces
coefficientwise positivity to
\[
c+m=4,\qquad x=0,\qquad\epsilon\in\{0,1\}.
\]
There are ten such polynomials.  All ten have been computed exactly;
each coefficient is nonnegative.  Hence \(P_\epsilon\succeq0\), then
\(F_\epsilon\succeq0\) by (1), and finally
\[
D_\epsilon(c,m,s,x)\ge0\qquad(s\ge-1)
\]
because the Newton basis \(\binom{s+1}{r}\) is nonnegative there.

The ten exact base certificates are in
`path_isolate_p4_general_layer_lift_newton_quotient_bases_20260730.json`.
They are regenerated by
`prove_path_isolate_p4_general_layer_lift_newton_quotient_bases.py`.
The finite audit of (1) contains 150 complete polynomials, 1,860
factor divisions, and 3,726 quotient coefficients.  The audit of
(2) contains 200 complete polynomials and 12,208 coefficient
comparisons.  These audits are evidence, not proofs of (1) or (2).

The significance of this reduction is that the previously
four-parameter, all-degree positivity problem has been replaced by
three explicit coefficientwise recurrences plus ten finished base
objects.

## Unconditional initial segment

coordinatewise recurrences, the first seven formal quotient
coefficients are now proved nonnegative on the full application
domain:
\[

\qquad(0\le r\le6,\ c+m\ge4).
\]
The first four have global positive-monomial certificates.  The fifth
is certified by the exact five-cone partition
\[

\]
All ten parity/cone numerator expansions are coefficientwise

`path_isolate_p4_general_layer_lift_quotient_order4_cones_20260730.json`.
