# Erdős Problem #993 literature refresh — 2026-08-30

## Current public status

The public literature located in an exact-title/conjecture search still treats the
Alavi–Malde–Schwenk–Erdős tree/forest unimodality statement as open.  No published
proof or finite counterexample to the full tree/forest statement was located.

This is a literature-status finding, not mathematical evidence that a resolution
cannot exist outside the indexed sources searched.

## Primary and current sources checked

1. **Erdős Problems, Problem 993.** The current indexed problem page continues to
   state “The independent set sequence of any tree or forest is unimodal” as the
   problem, with the 1987 Alavi–Malde–Schwenk–Erdős paper as its source.
   <https://www.erdosproblems.com/993>

2. **Grace M. X. Li, arXiv:2603.03025 (submitted 2026-03-03), _Unimodality of
   independence polynomials of two family of trees_.** The paper explicitly calls
   the full statement Conjecture 1.1 and proves unimodality only for the two named
   infinite families arising from the known non-log-concave constructions.
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03025>

3. **Takayuki Hibi, Selvi Kara, and Dalena Vien, arXiv:2604.18824 (submitted
   2026-04-20), _Symmetric and unimodal independence polynomials of trees_.** The
   stated scope concerns existence/construction of trees with symmetric unimodal
   independence polynomials, not all trees or forests.
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18824>

4. **Amire Bendjeddou and Lennart Hardiman, _Bulletin of the London Mathematical
   Society_ 57 (2025), 1305–1323, “Lorentzian polynomials and the independence
   sequences of graphs.”** The authors describe their pre-Lorentzian construction
   as progress on the conjecture, not a full resolution.
   <https://doi.org/10.1112/blms.70031>

5. **David Galvin, _Electronic Journal of Combinatorics_ 28(3) (2021), P3.23.**
   This peer-reviewed survey/result paper states that the full tree/forest question
   remains open and records the known decreasing-tail theorem.
   <https://doi.org/10.37236/9896>

6. **Public computational repository checked as non-peer-reviewed evidence.** The
   August 2026 README explicitly says that it does not contain a proof of Problem
   993 and lists narrower active targets.
   <https://github.com/BrettRey/erdos-problem-993>

## Search boundary

Searches covered the exact conjecture names, “Erdős Problem #993,” tree/forest
independence-polynomial unimodality, and 2025–2026 arXiv results.  Computational
census claims and repository work were not promoted to proof.  Before any final
manuscript claim of novelty, repeat the search and check citation graphs for the
2026 papers.
