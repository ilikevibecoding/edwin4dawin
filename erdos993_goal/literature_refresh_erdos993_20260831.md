# Erdős Problem #993 literature refresh — 2026-08-31

## Question checked

Whether the 1987 Alavi–Malde–Schwenk–Erdős conjecture that every tree or
forest has a unimodal independence sequence has already been proved or
disproved in the public literature.

## Search conclusion

No prior full resolution was located in the sources and searches below.  The
strongest current evidence found continues to describe the universal statement
as a conjecture and proves only special families.  This is a literature-search
result, not a proof that no unpublished or unindexed resolution exists.

## Current primary-source evidence

- Grace M. X. Li, *Unimodality of independence polynomials of two family of
  trees*, arXiv:2603.03025 (3 March 2026), states the universal forest claim as
  Conjecture 1.1 and proves unimodality only for the two infinite families
  `T_{3,m,n}` and `T^*_{3,m,n}`:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03025>
- Takayuki Hibi, Selvi Kara, and Dalena Vien, *Symmetric and unimodal
  independence polynomials of trees*, arXiv:2604.18824 (20 April 2026), studies
  the existence of trees with symmetric unimodal independence polynomials; its
  abstract does not claim the universal conjecture:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18824>
- Amire Bendjeddou and Lennart Hardiman, *Lorentzian polynomials and the
  independence sequences of graphs*, *Bulletin of the London Mathematical
  Society* 57 (2025), 1305–1323, describes its result as progress on the
  Alavi–Malde–Schwenk–Erdős conjecture and proves a special operator image:
  <https://doi.org/10.1112/blms.70031>
- Yuan Xie and Xiaoqian Zhang, *Unimodality of Independence Polynomials of
  Rooted Products of a Kind of Tree*, *Mathematical Theory and Applications*
  45(2) (2025), 53–75, explicitly says the conjecture is still open and proves
  a rooted-product class:
  <https://mta.csu.edu.cn/EN/10.3969/j.issn.1006-8074.2025.02.004>
- The current Erdős Problems entry #993 still records the universal tree/forest
  statement and cites the 1987 source:
  <https://www.erdosproblems.com/993>

## Search scope

The refresh searched the public web and arXiv for combinations of
`Alavi Malde Schwenk Erdős`, `independence polynomial`, `independent set
sequence`, `tree`, `forest`, `unimodal`, `proof`, `counterexample`, and 2026
arXiv month prefixes through August 2026.  It separately checked the current
Erdős Problems entry and the newest directly relevant arXiv results returned.

## Boundary for the proof project

The literature refresh does not reduce the proof obligation.  A project result
must still supply either a complete universal proof with independently
replayable exact certificates for every computational reduction, or a finite
explicit forest counterexample whose independence sequence can be recomputed
directly.
