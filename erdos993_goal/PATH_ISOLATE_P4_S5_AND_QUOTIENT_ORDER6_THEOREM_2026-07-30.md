## Seventh support value

For
\[
D_\epsilon(c,m,s,x)=
G_\epsilon(c,m+1,s,x)-G_\epsilon(c,m,s,x),
\]
one has
\[
D_\epsilon(c,m,5,x)\ge0
\]
for all \(c,m,x\ge0\) with \(c+m\ge4\), and both parities.

After clearing the positive central-binomial and \(m\)-denominator
factors, the even and odd numerator polynomials have degree bounds
\[
(14,22,12),\qquad(13,20,11)
\]
in \((c,m,x)\).  Exact interpolation used 5,376 even and 4,290 odd
grid points.  The global tensor Newton expansions contain 1,693 and
1,339 nonzero coefficients.  Three even global coefficients are
negative, all arising from the irrelevant region \(c+m<4\).

Partitioning the application domain into
\[
(0,4+M),(1,3+M),(2,2+M),(3,1+M),(4+C,M)
\]
removes that artifact.  On every one of the ten parity/cone cases,
the tensor Newton coefficients are nonnegative.  The exact
certificate is
`path_isolate_p4_boundary_s5_cone_certificate_20260730.json`.

## Seventh quotient coefficient

Let
\[
F_\epsilon(z)=
\sum_{r\ge0}\Delta_s^r
\left.
\left(
\frac{D_\epsilon(c,m,s,x)}
{\binom{2m+\epsilon}{m}}
\right)\right|_{s=-1}z^r
\]
and formally write
\[
F_\epsilon(z)=(1+z)^{2c+2m+x-1}
\sum_{r\ge0}B_{\epsilon,r}z^r.
\]
Then
\[
B_{\epsilon,6}(c,m,x)\ge0
\qquad(c+m\ge4).
\]

Four of the five cones in each parity have direct nonnegative tensor
Newton certificates.  The only exceptional coarse cone is
\(c=0,m=4+M\).  It is resolved as follows:

- even parity: the two-variable tensor Newton tail is nonnegative for
  \(m\ge11\); the slices \(4\le m\le10\) are covered by univariate
  \(x\)-tails, with respectively needed shifts
  \(0,0,9,15,17,12,5\), and every preceding \(x\)-value is positive;
- odd parity: the two-variable tail is nonnegative for \(m\ge8\);
  the slices \(m=4,5,6,7\) need \(x\)-shifts \(0,0,2,1\), with all
  preceding values positive.

Every tail certificate is an exact nonnegative tensor Newton
expansion.  Every finite initial value is an exact positive rational
number.  The complete replayable record is
`path_isolate_p4_general_layer_lift_order6_sparse_20260730.json`,
generated by
`prove_path_isolate_p4_general_layer_lift_order5_sparse.py --order 6`.

Thus the first seven formal quotient coefficients
\[
B_{\epsilon,r}\ge0\qquad(0\le r\le6)
\]
are rigorously proved throughout the parameter domain needed for the
general lift.
