# Stable path P4: bottom-pair Newton finite-base reduction

Let

\[
D_\epsilon(m,s,x)
=B_\epsilon(m+1,s,x)-B_\epsilon(m,s,x),
\]

where

\[
B_\epsilon(m,s,x)
=H_q^L(2m+\epsilon,0)+H_q^L(2m+\epsilon,1),
\quad
q=m+s+2,\quad L=2q-4+x.
\]

Form the Newton polynomial

\[
F_\epsilon(m,x;z)
=\sum_{r\ge0}
\left.\Delta_s^rD_\epsilon(m,s,x)\right|_{s=-1}z^r.
\]

## Sufficient uniform statements

It is enough to prove

\[
F_\epsilon(m,x;z)
=z(1+z)^{2m+x-1}P_\epsilon(m,x;z)
\tag{1}
\]

and the two coefficientwise recurrences

\[
P_\epsilon(m+1,x;z)
\succeq P_\epsilon(m,x;z),
\tag{2}
\]

\[
P_\epsilon(m,x+1;z)
\succeq P_\epsilon(m,x;z)
\tag{3}
\]

for \(m\ge3,\ x\ge0\), and both parities.

Indeed, (2)--(3) reduce every parameter pair to \(m=3,x=0\).
The two base polynomials are explicitly positive.

For even parity their 18 coefficients, in increasing powers of
\(z\), are

\[
\begin{aligned}
&(61508,\ 4317672,\ 104675596,\ 1522956758,\\
&16059845888,\ 122744394358,\ 664017638114,\\
&2548967968628,\ 7050685695290,\ 14267647111996,\\
&21321336934424,\ 23564163642156,\ 19099897720080,\\
&11112428944712,\ 4450863510056,\ 1134024906952,\\
&155775169536,\ 7237004096).
\end{aligned}
\]

For odd parity their 20 coefficients are

\[
\begin{aligned}
&(6608,\ 1603724,\ 70857042,\ 1466882290,\\
&20264466080,\ 207578020286,\ 1557559299494,\\
&8436096256428,\ 33189884802314,\ 96295269118884,\\
&209033485648986,\ 342858572999508,\\
&426499891154172,\ 400975690446172,\\
&281406783769252,\ 143825988370568,\\
&51214211362560,\ 11717701783896,\\
&1458183970656,\ 61969854960).
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus (1)--(3) imply \(P_\epsilon\succeq0\).  Both \(z\) and
\((1+z)^{2m+x-1}\) have nonnegative coefficients, so every Newton
coefficient of \(D_\epsilon\) is nonnegative.  Since
\(\binom{s+1}{r}\ge0\) for \(s\ge-1\), this proves

\[
B_\epsilon(m+1,s,x)\ge B_\epsilon(m,s,x)
\]

uniformly.

## Exact evidence

The complete-polynomial audit used

\[
m\in\{4,5,6,8,10\},\qquad
x\in\{0,4,12,32,45\},
\]

and both parities.  Across 50 polynomials it made 3,930 Newton
coefficient checks, 1,540 exact divisions by \(1+z\), and 2,240
quotient coefficient checks.  Every polynomial had exactly the
factor in (1), including one factor \(z\), and every quotient
coefficient was positive.

The recurrence audit computed 84 complete polynomials on
\(3\le m\le8,\ 0\le x\le6\), and made 3,940 coefficient
comparisons for (2)--(3), with no failure.  The two base
polynomials above were independently included in that audit.

The replayable records are
`path_isolate_p4_bottom_pair_lift_newton_stress_20260730.json` and
`path_isolate_p4_bottom_pair_quotient_monotonicity_stress_20260730.json`.
The factorization and recurrences are still uniform proof
obligations; the finite-base implication itself is exact.

## Initial uniform coefficient theorem

The first five quotient coefficients

\[
[z^k]P_\epsilon(m,x;z),\qquad k=0,1,2,3,4,
\]

and each of their unnormalized \(m\)- and \(x\)-coordinate
recurrences are now proved uniformly for \(m\ge3,\ x\ge0\), in both
parities.  Fixed-support symbolic evaluation expresses all 18
quantities as rational functions after \(m=3+M\).  Every denominator
has nonnegative coefficients and every numerator has nonnegative
ordinary monomial coefficients.

For even parity, the order-\(4\) coefficient, \(m\)-recurrence, and
\(x\)-recurrence numerators have respectively 135, 145, and 117
terms.  For odd parity the corresponding counts are 108, 117, and
92.  Together with orders \(0,1,2,3\), every one of the 30 rational
certificates has nonnegative numerator and denominator
coefficients.

The numerator degree pattern is exact throughout the proved front.
At quotient order \(k\), it is

\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
&P_k&\Delta_m P_k&\Delta_x P_k\\ \hline
\epsilon=0&
(3k+5,\,2k+1)&(3k+6,\,2k+1)&(3k+4,\,2k)\\
\epsilon=1&
(3k+3,\,2k)&(3k+4,\,2k)&(3k+2,\,2k-1)
\end{array}
\]

in \((M,x)\), with the final odd \(x\)-recurrence equal to zero at
\(k=0\).  This linear degree law is additional structural evidence
that a single all-order coefficient formula should exist.

The complete formulas and hashes are in
`path_isolate_p4_bottom_pair_initial_quotient_order0_to_4_20260730.json`,
generated by
`prove_path_isolate_p4_bottom_pair_initial_quotient.py --max-order 4`.

The same proved front satisfies a stronger pair of recurrences:
\[
\begin{aligned}
P_\epsilon(m+1,x;z)&\succeq
(1+2z)^2P_\epsilon(m,x;z),\\
P_\epsilon(m,x+1;z)&\succeq
(1+2z)P_\epsilon(m,x;z).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
For \(k=0,1,2,3,4\), the coefficient forms of both inequalities
have positive-coefficient rational certificates after normalizing by
the old central binomial and shifting \(m=3+M\).  This gives 20 more
uniform certificates.  The replayable record is
`path_isolate_p4_bottom_pair_initial_multiplicative_recurrence_order0_to_4_20260730.json`.

Finite complete-polynomial evidence for (4) covers 84 polynomials and
3,940 coefficient comparisons on \(3\le m\le8,\ 0\le x\le6\), with
no failure.  The multiplier \(1+2z\) per added stable-path vertex is
the sharpest structural pattern currently visible in the quotient
recurrences.
