# Stable path P4: all-order positivity of the grouped `P` tail

Date: 2026-08-01

This note proves one of the two pieces in the grouped-tail decomposition of
the positive-intersection coordinate recurrences.  It applies in the main
stable region

\[
c\ge 1,\qquad m\ge 3,\qquad x\ge 0,
\]

and in both path parities \(\epsilon\in\{0,1\}\).

## 1. Finite numerator and grouped tail

Put

\[
q=zw,\quad A=(1+z)(1+w),\quad V=1+z+w,
\quad T=z(1+z)+w(1+w).
\]

For each coordinate recurrence \(d\in\{x,c,m\}\), the exact rational
generating numerator has normalized coefficient kernels
\(C_{d,0},\ldots,C_{d,J_d}\), where

\[
J_x=3,\qquad J_c=J_m=5.
\]

At Newton order \(k\), the finite-numerator contribution is

\[
S_{d,k}=\sum_{j=0}^{J_d}(k-j+1)C_{d,j}V^{k-j}.
\tag{1}
\]

Define

\[
P_d=\sum_{j=0}^{J_d}C_{d,j}V^{J_d-j},
\qquad
B_d=\sum_{j=0}^{J_d}(J_d-j+1)C_{d,j}V^{J_d-j}.
\tag{2}
\]

Then for every \(r\ge0\), direct collection of (1) gives

\[
S_{d,J_d+r}=V^r(B_d+rP_d).
\tag{3}
\]

The theorem below proves the nonnegative diagonal contribution of the
entire \(rV^rP_d\) term, uniformly in \(r\) and all parameters.  The
remaining problem is therefore confined to \(V^rB_d\).

## 2. Reciprocal variables and the paired cone

For a symmetric polynomial \(F\) of bidegree \((D,D)\), write

\[
F^\vee(z,w)=z^Dw^D F(1/z,1/w).
\]

The relevant reciprocal factors are

\[
A^\vee=A,
\qquad
V^\vee=W:=z+w+zw,
\qquad
T^\vee=S:=z^2+w^2+zw(z+w).
\tag{4}
\]

Let \(e_1=z+w\), \(p_2=z^2+w^2\).  Call a symmetric polynomial HCU if
every homogeneous coefficient row rises weakly from the edge to the
center.  Equivalently, it has a nonnegative two-variable Schur expansion.

Call \(Q\) a member of the **paired cone** when each of its
parameter-homogeneous layers is a sum of an HCU polynomial and
nonnegative atoms

\[
q^a p_2^b\qquad(a,b\ge0).
\tag{5}
\]

## 3. Closure lemma

If \(Q\) is in the paired cone, then

\[
e_1 Q S^R
\tag{6}
\]

is HCU for every \(R\ge0\).  Indeed,

\[
S^R=(p_2+qe_1)^R
=\sum_{\ell=0}^R\binom R\ell
q^\ell e_1^\ell p_2^{R-\ell}.
\]

For the HCU portion of \(Q\), every summand in (6) contains the HCU
factor \(e_1^{\ell+1}p_2^{R-\ell}\).  For an atom \(q^ap_2^b\), the
corresponding summand is

\[
q^{a+\ell}e_1^{\ell+1}p_2^{b+R-\ell},
\]

which is HCU by the paired-binomial lemma.  HCU polynomials are closed
under nonnegative sums and products.  The additional factors \(A\) and
\(W=e_1+q\) are HCU, so arbitrary nonnegative powers of them preserve
the conclusion.

## 4. Exact six-kernel certificate

Make the nonnegative parameter shift

\[
c=1+C,\qquad m=3+M,
\qquad C,M,x\ge0.
\]

For both parities and all three coordinates, exact sparse division gives

\[
P_d^\vee=e_1Q_d,
\tag{7}
\]

where every parameter-homogeneous layer of \(Q_d\) is HCU except for one
parameter-free layer.  That layer has the following explicit paired-cone
certificate:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
d&\text{exception atom}&\text{Schur-nonnegative remainder}\\ \hline
x&2p_2^9&Q_x-2p_2^9\\
c&4qp_2^9&Q_c-4qp_2^9\\
m&qp_2^{11}&Q_m-qp_2^{11}.
\end{array}
\tag{8}
\]

The same three atoms work in both parities.  Thus all six \(Q_d\) belong
to the paired cone.

## 5. All-order consequence

In the original coefficient extraction, the multiplier of \(P_d\) has
the form

\[
A^{2c+m+x-3}T^{2m+\epsilon-4}V^r.
\]

All exponents are nonnegative in the stated main region.  After
reciprocity, (4) and (7) turn this product into

\[
A^{2c+m+x-3}S^{2m+\epsilon-4}W^r e_1Q_d,
\]

which is HCU by the closure lemma.  Hence its diagonal coefficient is
nonnegative.  Multiplication by the scalar \(r\ge0\) preserves this.

Therefore, for every Newton-tail index \(r\ge0\), every admissible
\(c,m,x\), both parities, and all three coordinate recurrences, the
complete \(rV^rP_d\) contribution in (3) is nonnegative.

This theorem does **not** yet prove the signed base contribution
\(V^rB_d\); that is the remaining infinite-tail obstruction.
