# Terminal q3 Newton m=0: arbitrary double-star tail

Date: 2026-08-31

Let the no-isolate remainder be the sorted double star `D_(a,b)`, with
`a>=b>=2`.  Its exact independence and one-edge rows are generated by

```text
F(x)=(1+x)^(a+b)+x(1+x)^a+x(1+x)^b,
Z(x)=x^2(1+a(1+x)^b+b(1+x)^a).
```

For every target `j>=b+3`, all high rows contributed by the smaller centre
vanish.  After dividing by `B=C(a+b,j)`, the exact Newton `m=0` margin is
affine in the sole remaining mixture weight

```text
rho=C(a,j-1)/C(a+b,j).
```

When this row is active,

```text
0 <= rho <= j(a+b-j)/((a+b)(a+b-1)).
```

The right endpoint is exact at `b=2`; every additional hypergeometric product
factor lies in `[0,1]`.  The lower endpoint reduces to a polynomial with 262
strictly positive monomials, and the upper endpoint to one with 423 strictly
positive monomials.  Both minimum coefficients are one, and every denominator
is strictly positive on its cone.  Affinity then closes the full interval.

If `a<=j-3`, `rho=0` exactly.  At the sole seam `a=j-2`, the true
`f_(j-1)` row has one extra positive contribution.  The exact anchor
determinant has 20 strictly positive monomials, so the lower endpoint remains
a valid lower bound there.

This proves every target `j>=b+3` for every double star.  It does not yet
close the middle targets `4<=j<=b+2` when `b>=3`, other forest remainders,
nonisolated marked roots, general terminal `m=0`, or Erdős Problem #993.
