# Near-full-intersection P4 group on stable paths

Date: 2026-07-30

In the fixed-intersection decomposition of the stable path P4
coefficient, the group

\[
h=j-1
\]

is nonnegative for every \(j\ge1\), \(q\ge5\), and
\(L\ge2q-4\).

For \(q\le j\) the group is zero.  On the nonzero unbounded range
\(j\ge4,\ q\ge j+1\), put

\[
j=4+k,\qquad q=j+1+s,\qquad L=2q-4+x.
\]

The group equals

\[
j\{Q(j-1,j)+Q(j,j-1)\}.
\]

After division by the positive stable P4 factorial factor, exact
simplification gives a second positive factorial ratio times a
polynomial in \(k,s,x\).  This polynomial has degree
\((16,13,13)\), 1,199 nonzero monomials, no negative coefficient,
and smallest coefficient \(8\).

The small layers \(j=1,2,3\) have separate fixed-layer exact
certificates.  The all-layer derivation is generated by
`prove_path_isolate_p4_near_full_intersection_all_layers.py`.
