# Terminal q3 Newton m=0: double stars with smaller side two

Date: 2026-08-31

Let the no-isolate remainder be the double star `D_(a,2)`, with `a>=2`.
Its exact rows are generated by

```text
F(x)=(1+x)^(a+2)+x(1+x)^a+x(1+x)^2,
Z(x)=x^2(1+a(1+x)^2+2(1+x)^a).
```

The arbitrary-forest `j=3` theorem closes the first target.  At `j=4`, the
remaining exceptional low row gives a one-variable polynomial that is
coefficientwise positive after `a=2+x`.  For `j>=5`, put

```text
a=j+x-2,  j=5+y,  x,y>=0.
```

The exact margin divided by `f_j` becomes a positive rational prefactor
times a polynomial with 54 strictly positive monomials and minimum
coefficient one.  Its denominator is a product of polynomials that are
strictly positive throughout the cone.  Thus the Newton `m=0` margin is
positive for every supported target in this family.

The certificate also reconstructs every literal integer row through large
side 600 and checks all supported targets.  This does not promote arbitrary
double stars, general marked-isolate remainders, general terminal `m=0`, or
Erdős Problem #993.
