# Full-intersection P4 group on stable paths

Date: 2026-07-30

In the fixed-intersection decomposition of the stable path P4
coefficient, let \(j\) be the input isolate layer and let
\(h=|A\cap B|\).  Then the extreme group

\[
h=j
\]

is nonnegative for every \(j\ge0\), \(q\ge5\), and
\(L\ge2q-4\).

For \(q\le j\), every phase product contains a negative-rank row
after the \(j\) common selected isolates are removed, so this group
is zero.

For \(j\ge4\) and \(q\ge j+1\), set

\[
j=4+k,\qquad q=j+1+s=5+k+s,\qquad L=2q-4+x.
\]

After division by the usual positive stable P4 factorial factor, the
exact group is a second positive factorial ratio times a polynomial
in \(k,s,x\).  That polynomial has degree \((12,12,10)\), 607
nonzero monomials, no negative coefficient, and smallest coefficient
\(4\).  This proves the entire unbounded \(h=j\) edge at once.

The cases \(j=0,1,2\) are covered by the existing stable transition
and fixed-intersection certificates; \(j=3\) has a separate exact
fixed-layer replay.

The derivation and compact certificate are generated by
`prove_path_isolate_p4_full_intersection_all_layers.py` and stored in
`path_isolate_p4_full_intersection_all_layers_20260730.json`.
