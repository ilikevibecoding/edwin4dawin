Let
\[
D_\epsilon(c,m,s,x)=
G_\epsilon(c,m+1,s,x)-G_\epsilon(c,m,s,x)
\]
be the general two-layer lift residual, for \(s\ge-1\).  For a
sequence \(f\) on this range, put
\[
(\mathcal Sf)(s)=\sum_{t=-1}^{s}f(t).
\]

## Sufficient prefix inequalities

It is enough to prove the following three pointwise inequalities:
\[
\begin{aligned}
D_\epsilon(c+1,m,s,x)
&\ge \mathcal S^2D_\epsilon(c,m,\cdot,x)(s),\\
D_\epsilon(c,m+1,s,x)
&\ge \mathcal S^2D_\epsilon(c,m,\cdot,x)(s),\\
D_\epsilon(c,m,s,x+1)
&\ge \mathcal SD_\epsilon(c,m,\cdot,x)(s).
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]
They are required for \(c,m,x\ge0\), \(c+m\ge4\), both parities,
and all \(s\ge-1\).

Indeed, the ten exact base polynomials at
\[
c+m=4,\qquad x=0,\qquad \epsilon\in\{0,1\}
\]
prove \(D_\epsilon\ge0\) for every \(s\ge-1\).  Any admissible pair
\((c,m)\) coordinatewise dominates one of
\[
(0,4),(1,3),(2,2),(3,1),(4,0).
\]
Starting from that base pair, repeated use of the first two
inequalities in (1), and then the third, preserves nonnegativity and
reaches every \(c,m,x\) with \(c+m\ge4\).  Thus (1) proves the full
general two-layer lift.

## Relation to the Newton quotient

If
\[
f(s)=\sum_{r\ge0}A_r\binom{s+1}{r},
\qquad F(z)=\sum_{r\ge0}A_rz^r,
\]
then the Newton polynomial of \(\mathcal Sf\) is
\[
(1+z)F(z).
\]
This follows from the hockey-stick identity
\[
\sum_{t=-1}^{s}\binom{t+1}{r}
=\binom{s+2}{r+1}.
\]
Consequently, the previously observed coordinatewise monotonicity of
the reduced Newton quotient implies (1).  However, (1) is strictly
weaker and is all the finite-base induction actually needs.

An independent direct evaluation made 6,300 exact checks on
\[
0\le c\le4,\quad 0\le m\le6,\quad c+m\ge4,\quad
0\le x\le2,\quad -1\le s\le12
\]
for both parities and found no failure of (1).  The replayable audit
is `path_isolate_p4_prefix_lift_stress_20260730.json`, generated by
`stress_path_isolate_p4_prefix_lifts.py`.

The next proof target is therefore the sequence-level form (1), which
may be attacked directly by ordinary generating functions or by the
coefficient-extraction normal form, without proving every quotient
coefficient recurrence separately.

## Uniform boundary theorem

All three inequalities in (1) are now proved uniformly on the first
five support diagonals
\[
s=-1,0,1,2,3.
\]
Using the exact formulas for
\(D_\epsilon/\binom{2m+\epsilon}{m}\), the required prefix sums were
formed symbolically.  For the \(m\)-lift the central-binomial ratio is
\[
\frac{\binom{2m+2}{m+1}}{\binom{2m}{m}}
=\frac{2(2m+1)}{m+1}
\]
in even parity and
\[
\frac{\binom{2m+3}{m+1}}{\binom{2m+1}{m}}
=\frac{2(2m+3)}{m+2}
\]
in odd parity.  Each of the resulting 30 rational functions has a
positive denominator and a numerator with nonnegative coefficients
in \(c,m,x\).  There are no negative coefficients.

`path_isolate_p4_prefix_lift_boundary_20260730.json`, generated by
`prove_path_isolate_p4_prefix_lift_boundary.py`.
