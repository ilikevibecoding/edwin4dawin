# Path–isolate P4: initial Newton-coefficient theorem

> **Scope warning.**  The proved initial coefficients remain valid,
> but the full Newton polynomial is not coefficientwise nonnegative
> in general.  The underlying lift fails exactly at
> \((c,m,s,x,\epsilon)=(0,8,6,45,0)\); see
> `PATH_ISOLATE_P4_GENERAL_LAYER_LIFT_COUNTEREXAMPLE_2026-07-30.md`.

Put
\[
G_\epsilon(c,m,s,x)=
\sum_{u=0}^{2m+\epsilon}\binom{2m+\epsilon}{u}
Q_{c+m+s+2}^{\,2(c+m+s+2)-4+x}
(c+u,c+2m+\epsilon-u)
\]
and
\[
D_\epsilon(c,m,s,x)=
G_\epsilon(c,m+1,s,x)-G_\epsilon(c,m,s,x).
\]
The desired general two-layer lift is \(D_\epsilon\ge0\).

For fixed \(c,m,x,\epsilon\), expand the residual in the Newton
basis based at \(s=-1\):
\[
\frac{D_\epsilon(c,m,s,x)}
{\binom{2m+\epsilon}{m}}
=\sum_{r\ge0} A_{\epsilon,r}(c,m,x)
\binom{s+1}{r}.
\]
Then
\[
A_{\epsilon,r}(c,m,x)\ge0
\qquad(0\le r\le4)
\]
for every \(c,m,x\ge0\) and \(\epsilon\in\{0,1\}\).

Indeed,
\[
A_{\epsilon,r}
=\Delta_s^r\left.
\left(
\frac{D_\epsilon(c,m,s,x)}
{\binom{2m+\epsilon}{m}}
\right)\right|_{s=-1}.
\]
The exact boundary formulas at \(s=-1,0,1,2,3\) from
`PATH_ISOLATE_P4_GENERAL_LAYER_LIFT_BOUNDARY_THEOREM_2026-07-30.md`
therefore determine \(A_{\epsilon,r}\) for \(r\le4\).  Direct exact
differencing gives positive denominators and numerator polynomials
in \(c,m,x\) with no negative coefficients.  Their degrees and term
counts are:

This is not yet a proof for every \(r\).  Its importance is structural:

## The first five coefficients after the common factor

Write the formal quotient
\[
\sum_{r\ge0}A_{\epsilon,r}z^r
=(1+z)^{2c+2m+x-1}
\sum_{r\ge0}B_{\epsilon,r}z^r.
\]
For the application one has \(c+m\ge4\).  Then
\[
B_{\epsilon,r}(c,m,x)\ge0
\qquad(0\le r\le4)
\]
for both parities.

Orders \(0,\ldots,3\) have global positive-monomial certificates.
For order \(4\), partition the admissible domain into
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
c=0,\ m=4+M;&c=1,\ m=3+M;\\
c=2,\ m=2+M;&c=3,\ m=1+M;\\
c=4+C,\ m=M,
\end{array}
\qquad C,M\ge0.
\]
After each substitution, the numerator of
\(B_{\epsilon,4}\) has nonnegative ordinary monomial coefficients.
There are ten parity/cone cases, with zero negative coefficients;
the smallest numerator coefficient is \(96\).

The exact order-four cone certificate is
`path_isolate_p4_general_layer_lift_quotient_order4_cones_20260730.json`,
generated by
`prove_path_isolate_p4_general_layer_lift_quotient_order4_cones.py`.
Thus the first five coefficients of the conjectural quotient, not
only of the unfactored Newton polynomial, are rigorously settled on
the full parameter domain needed by the proof.

## Sixth coefficient

On the application domain \(c+m\ge4\), both the sixth unfactored
Newton coefficient and the sixth formal quotient coefficient are
nonnegative:
\[
A_{\epsilon,5}(c,m,x)\ge0,\qquad
B_{\epsilon,5}(c,m,x)\ge0.
\]
The proof uses the five cones
\[
(0,4+M),(1,3+M),(2,2+M),(3,1+M),(4+C,M)
\]
and a tensor Newton basis in each cone.  All ten parity/cone
certificates have zero negative coefficients.  Ordinary monomials
already suffice in every quotient case except the even
\((c,m)=(0,4+M)\) cone; its two negative ordinary coefficients are
absorbed in the tensor Newton basis, whose coefficients are all
nonnegative.

The computation uses the direct identity
\[
B_r=\sum_{i=0}^{r}
(-1)^{r-i}\binom{E+r}{r-i}
\frac{D_\epsilon(c,m,i-1,x)}
{\binom{2m+\epsilon}{m}},
\qquad E=2c+2m+x-1.
\]
Its proof is recorded in
`PATH_ISOLATE_P4_QUOTIENT_COEFFICIENT_IDENTITY_2026-07-30.md`.

`path_isolate_p4_general_layer_lift_order5_sparse_20260730.json`,
generated by
