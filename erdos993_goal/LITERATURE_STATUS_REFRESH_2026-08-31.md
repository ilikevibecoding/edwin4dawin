# Erdős Problem #993 literature-status refresh

Date checked: 2026-08-31

## Result

No prior complete proof and no finite nonunimodal tree or forest was located in
the current search.  The public literature located in this refresh continues to
treat the Alavi--Malde--Schwenk--Erdős tree/forest unimodality conjecture as
open.  This is a literature-search result, not a proof that no unpublished or
unindexed resolution exists, so a fresh search is still required immediately
before any public resolution claim.

## Primary and official records checked

1. Erdős Problems, Problem #993:
   https://www.erdosproblems.com/latex/993

   The official record states the tree/forest independent-set-sequence
   unimodality problem and cites the 1987 source.  The record contains no proof
   or counterexample resolution.

2. Brett Reynolds, *Mean bounds, structural reductions, and exhaustive
   verification for tree independence polynomial unimodality*, Zenodo record
   19100781, version dated 2026-03-18:
   https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19100781

   The record explicitly says the conjecture remains open.  It reports
   exhaustive unimodality verification for all 8,691,747,673 trees on at most
   29 vertices and several structural partial results; none is a full proof.

3. Grace M. X. Li, *Unimodality of independence polynomials of two family of
   trees*, arXiv:2603.03025, submitted 2026-03-03:
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03025

   The paper states the general forest conjecture as Conjecture 1.1 and proves
   unimodality only for the two named infinite families `T_(3,m,n)` and
   `T*_(3,m,n)`.

4. Takayuki Hibi, Selvi Kara, and Dalena Vien, *Symmetric and unimodal
   independence polynomials of trees*, arXiv:2604.18824, submitted 2026-04-20:
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18824

   The introduction explicitly says that, as of April 2026, the conjecture
   remains open in general.  Its theorems construct particular symmetric and
   unimodal tree independence polynomials; they do not resolve every tree.

5. César Bautista-Ramos, Carlos Guillén-Galván, and Paulino Gómez-Salgado,
   *Linear Recurrences for Non-Log-Concave Independence Polynomials of Trees*,
   Graphs and Combinatorics 42, article 59 (published 2026-06-22):
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s00373-026-03054-4

   This paper constructs and analyzes failures of log-concavity.  It describes
   unimodality as the surviving conjecture and does not give a nonunimodal tree.
   A log-concavity failure is not a unimodality failure.

## Search coverage and false-positive exclusions

Searches used the exact conjecture name, the four authors' names, Erdős Problem
#993, and combinations of "tree", "forest", "independence polynomial", and
"unimodal", with special attention to 2025--2026 arXiv and journal records.

One search index associated arXiv:2605.02193 with this conjecture.  Directly
opening that arXiv record shows that it is *Trees and Graphs with Non
Log-concave Dominating Set Sequence via AI Tools*, about dominating-set
sequences rather than independent-set sequences.  It is therefore not a
resolution or relevant counterexample to Problem #993.

Recent papers on non-log-concavity, symmetric examples, special families,
unicyclic graphs, and computational searches were not promoted to a resolution
unless they addressed every tree/forest or supplied a finite nonunimodal forest.
None located in this refresh met that bar.

## Resolution boundary

The literature requirement is currently satisfied only in the following
careful sense: the checked public record still reports the conjecture open and
no prior resolution was located.  The mathematical objective remains open in
this worktree until either the complete proof dependency chain is closed and
independently replayed or a finite forest counterexample is emitted and
independently verified.
