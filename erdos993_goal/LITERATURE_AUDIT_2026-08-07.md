# Literature audit for Erdős Problem #993

Audit date: 2026-08-07 (America/New_York)

Target statement: the independent-set sequence of every tree or forest is
unimodal (Alavi--Malde--Schwenk--Erdős, 1987).

## Current public status

- The Erdős Problems page labels #993 `Open` and reports no claimed partial
  or complete solution in its incorporated remarks or comments.  The page
  was last edited 2026-02-01:
  https://www.erdosproblems.com/993
- The active public computational repository for #993 states, under
  "Current public targets, July 2026", that it does not contain a proof and
  that the tree case remains open:
  https://github.com/BrettRey/erdos-problem-993

## Recent directly relevant papers checked

- Li, Li, Yang, Zhang, *A symmetric function approach to log-concavity of
  independence polynomials*, arXiv:2501.04245 (2025).  It describes the
  all-tree conjecture as open and proves log-concavity for spiders and
  pineapple graphs:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.04245
- Li, *Unimodality of independence polynomials of two family of trees*,
  arXiv:2603.03025 (2026).  It proves the conjectured conclusion for the two
  Kadrawi--Levit infinite tree families, not for all trees:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03025
- Hibi, Kara, Vien, *Symmetric and unimodal independence polynomials of
  trees*, arXiv:2604.18824 (2026).  It studies existence and construction of
  trees having symmetric unimodal independence polynomials, not the
  universal conjecture:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18824
- Kadrawi and Levit, *The independence polynomial of trees is not always
  log-concave starting from order 26*, arXiv:2305.01784 (2023).  It refutes
  the stronger log-concavity conjecture while its exhibited trees remain
  unimodal:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.01784
- Yosef, Mizrachi, Kadrawi, *On Unimodality of Independence Polynomials of
  Trees*, arXiv:2101.06744 (2021).  It checks trees through 20 vertices and
  presents this as evidence for the still-open conjecture:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.06744

## Search scope and conclusion

Queries covered the exact conjecture wording, its four originators, Erdős
Problem #993, `independence polynomial`, `independent set sequence`, `tree`,
`forest`, `unimodal`, recent arXiv records, the Erdős Problems discussion,
and the active public repository.  Results through 2026-08-07 include newer
family theorems and much larger finite searches, but no universal proof and
no finite counterexample.

Conclusion: as of this audit, #993 is still an appropriate open target.
This is a literature audit, not a guarantee that no uncatalogued manuscript
or unpublished result exists.  Any eventual proof should receive a second
independent search (MathSciNet/Zentralblatt/Crossref/Google Scholar where
available) and expert review before a novelty claim.
