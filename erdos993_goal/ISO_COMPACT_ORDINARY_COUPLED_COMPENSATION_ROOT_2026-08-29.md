# Exact source-paired compensation identity for the ordinary ISO gap

## Identity

Let \(X\) denote multiplication of every row in a four-minor tuple by
\(x\), and let \(B_N,B_R\) be the symmetric polarizations of the quadratic
forms \(N,R\).  For the ordinary support resolution

\[
A=C+XH,\qquad \mathrm{Full}=A+XC,
\]

define

\[
G_N(C,H)=N(C+XH+XC)-N(C+XH)-zwN(C).
\]

Exact quadratic polarization gives the source-paired identity

\[
\boxed{G_N(C,H)=L_N(C)+2B_N(XC,XH)},
\]

where

\[
L_N(C)=N((1+X)C)-N(C)-zwN(C).
\]

Writing \(\delta=(z-w)^2/2\), the multiplication rule
\(N(XT)=zwN(T)-\delta R(T)\) and its polarization give

\[
L_N(C)=(z+w)N(C)-\delta R(C),
\]

\[
B_N(XC,XH)=zwB_N(C,H)-\delta B_R(C,H).
\]

Adding these two paired expressions recovers exactly

\[
G_N=(z+w)N(C)+2zwB_N(C,H)
-\delta\,[R(C+H)-R(H)].
\]

Thus each signed \(R\)-curvature is not an independent defect: it is exactly
the correction generated by the same multiplication by \(X\).  Any bound
that separates those corrections loses the algebraic compensation.

## Scope and replay

This is an exact polynomial identity, not a positivity theorem.  In fact,
the two displayed source-paired summands still cannot be proved positive
separately; the companion order-seven certificate records the first
counterexample.
