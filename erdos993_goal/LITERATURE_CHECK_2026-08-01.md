# Erdős Problem 993 literature/status refresh — 2026-08-01

The public status remains open as of 2026-08-01.

## Primary live status

- The Erdős Problems discussion page labels #993 **Open** and reports no
  partial or complete solution claimed in its comments:
  https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/993

## Recent papers checked

- Grace M. X. Li, *Unimodality of independence polynomials of two family
  of trees*, arXiv:2603.03025 (2026), proves the conjecture for two
  infinite families arising from the known non-log-concave examples; it
  does not claim the full conjecture:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03025
- Takayuki Hibi, Selvi Kara, and Dalena Vien, *Symmetric and unimodal
  independence polynomials of trees*, arXiv:2604.18824 (2026), studies
  existence/construction questions for symmetric unimodal tree
  independence polynomials; it does not claim the full conjecture:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18824

## Recent computational report

- A 2026-07-28 SciNet report claims exhaustive unimodality verification
  for forests through 30 vertices and a finite closure statement.  It is
  labeled as awaiting independent review and does not resolve the
  unbounded conjecture:
  https://api.scinet.pub/f/25b8f9a4-efab-4707-9b77-18d3702ecbb3

This refresh found no public proof or counterexample to the full tree or
forest conjecture.  Absence from this search is not a proof of priority;
any final result still requires a broader bibliographic and author-level
priority check before circulation.
