# Terminal upper-unit cross bound and NCL square absorption

## Status

**Upper-unit cross bound refuted.**  The identities and conditional
implication in this note are proved, but a rigorous finite \(m=100\)
star-fork tree has

\[
\zeta=1.000533005878639\ldots>1.
\]

The full-square reserve, NCL, and C12 margins are all positive there.
Thus the counterexample refutes only the shortcut developed in this
note.  This is not a solution of Erdős Problem 993.

A rigorous finite star-fork certificate additionally refutes the older
standalone terminal-drift component (B).  The combined upper-unit
bound survives that counterexample, so it must be attacked without
separating the two drift components.

## 1. Upper-unit cross candidate

Use the live negative-cross notation

\[
r=k-1,\qquad
u=r\frac b{b^-},\qquad
v=k\frac {a^+}{a},\qquad
\zeta=v-\frac kr u>0.
\]

The now-refuted candidate was

\[
\boxed{\zeta\le1.}
\tag{UU}
\]

It is strictly weaker than both the refuted cross-ratio condition
\(\zeta\le0\) and the refuted one-step terminal drift condition.
Its exact coefficient form is

\[
\boxed{
a b^--k(b^-a^+-ab)\ge0.
}
\tag{1}
\]

The executable `verify_terminal_upper_unit_absorption.py` verifies
this equivalence symbolically.

## 2. Common-moment forms

Use the common terminal-mixture moments

\[
D=u+r\pi,\qquad N=W_2+kZ,\qquad
m=\frac kr u.
\]

Then

\[
D(1-\zeta)=D(1+m)-N.
\tag{2}
\]

Let

\[
R_F=r+u^2-W_2
\]

be the lower ISO reserve and let

\[
M_B=\pi(u+1)-Z
\]

be the normalized terminal-drift component (B).  Exact cancellation
gives

\[
\boxed{
D(1-\zeta)
=R_F+u-r+\frac{u^2}{r}-\pi+kM_B.
}
\tag{3}
\]

Formula (3) explains why proving \(M_B\ge0\) was unnecessarily strong:
the ISO reserve and the rank correction can compensate a negative
component-(B) margin.

There is also a particularly short one-downlink form.  For the
residual forests \(H=F-N[K]\) and the pointed link \(J\), put

\[
d_K=\frac{2i_2(H)+r i_1(J)}{i_1(H)},\qquad
n_K=\frac{6i_3(H)+2k i_2(J)}{i_1(H)}.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
D(1-\zeta)
=\mathbb E_\mu\{(1+m)d_K-n_K\}.
}
\tag{4}
\]

After multiplying the local integrand by \(i_1(H)\), it is

\[
\boxed{
2(1+m)i_2(H)-6i_3(H)
+r(1+m)i_1(J)-2k i_2(J).
}
\tag{5}
\]

Unlike the full-square downlink, (5) uses only ranks at most three.

## 3. The NCL square is absorbed for free

The exact negative-cross reserve form is

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal N={}&
2kR_T-\frac C uR_F\\
&+k(r+2)(u-r)
\left(\frac1r+\frac su\right)\\
&+\zeta\left(r+2+\frac{r^2}{u}\right)
-2k\{s\delta+\theta\zeta^2\},
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]

where

\[
\theta=\frac r{D+r},\qquad D\ge u.
\]

The coefficient of the favorable linear term satisfies

\[
\begin{aligned}
r+2+\frac{r^2}{u}
-\frac{2kr}{u+r}
&=
\frac{(r+2)u+r^3/u}{u+r}\\
&>0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]

Since \(\theta\le r/(u+r)\), (7) proves

\[
r+2+\frac{r^2}{u}>2k\theta.
\tag{8}
\]

Consequently, under \(0\le\zeta\le1\),

\[
\zeta\left(r+2+\frac{r^2}{u}\right)
-2k\theta\zeta^2\ge0.
\tag{9}
\]

Thus (UU) removes the quadratic obstruction in NCL without requiring
the stronger full-square reserve \(R_T\ge\zeta^2\).  It remains only
to prove the linear reserve cascade

\[
\boxed{
2kR_T-\frac C uR_F
+k(r+2)(u-r)\left(\frac1r+\frac su\right)
-2ks\delta\ge0.
}
\tag{ULC}
\]

## 4. Exact finite evidence and counterexample

The all-rank exact audits give:

* every tree through order \(16\): 270 negative-cross checks, no
  failure of (UU);
* 5,000 PatternBoost trees: 655 negative-cross checks, no failure of
  (UU);
* the graph atlas plus 50,000 random graphs: 911 negative-cross
  checks, no failure of (UU).

All of those ordinary finite negative-cross checks are direct descents,
so they are evidence about (UU), not instances where NCL is required.
The live \(m=60\) star-fork interval certificate proves

\[
1-\zeta
=0.9069301459708300648\ldots>0
\]

and

\[
R_T-\zeta^2>1.2797428701136001\times10^{20}.
\]

The same certificate proves that the older component (B) has margin

\[
u+1-r\frac{c_r}{c_{r-1}}
=-1.6383093053882958\ldots<0.
\]

Therefore component (B) is false even for a finite tree and cannot
prove (UU).  The relevant certificate is
`star_fork_downward_counterexample_m60_20260729.json`, reproduced by
`verify_star_fork_downward_counterexample_interval.py`.

More decisively, the live \(m=100\) star-fork instance with

\[
t=\left\lfloor\frac{71}{50}2^{100}\right\rfloor
\]

has the rigorous interval

\[
1-\zeta=-0.0005330058786393495\ldots<0.
\]

At the same rank,

\[
R_T-\zeta^2>1.8000638523240858\times10^{32},
\]

\[
\mathcal N>2.504260459523643\times10^{64},
\]

and the outer C12 margin is positive.  The independently replayable
certificate is
`star_fork_upper_unit_counterexample_m100_20260729.json`, generated by
`verify_star_fork_upper_unit_counterexample_interval.py`.

The still weaker attempt to pay the NCL square using only its favorable
linear \(\zeta\)-term is also false.  In the finite \(m=190\) star-fork
instance with

\[
t=\left\lfloor\frac{37}{25}2^{190}\right\rfloor,
\]

the rational-interval certificate proves

\[
\zeta=2.897238540390924\ldots
\]

and a strictly negative standalone linear-minus-square surplus,

\[
-3.000100820841155\times10^{58}.
\]

Nevertheless, the unit-paid reserve cascade is positive by more than
\(1.5054500426513017\times10^{119}\), the full-square reserve is
positive by more than \(4.412802519976836\times10^{59}\), and both
NCL and outer C12 remain positive.  The exact replay is
`star_fork_square_absorption_counterexample_m190_20260729.json`,
generated by
`verify_star_fork_square_absorption_counterexample_interval.py`.

The nonforest split-graph family does violate (UU): at its recorded
rank-seven full-square failure, \(\zeta=14.3584\ldots\).  Hence any
proof of (UU) must use forest structure.

## 5. Consequence for the proof program

The rank-three reduction (4)--(5) is exact but cannot be made
nonnegative in general, because (UU) is false.  The \(m=190\)
certificate also rules out separating the favorable linear term from
the unit-paid reserve cascade.  The proof program therefore returns to
the stronger coupled reserve alternatives:

1. prove the full-square reserve \(R_T\ge\zeta^2\) together with its
   remaining linear reserve cascade; or
2. prove NCL directly, retaining the \(\zeta\)-linear term, square,
   and both ISO reserves in one inequality.

The \(m=100\) certificate is an important sharpness test for any
replacement: a valid proof cannot cap the terminal cross displacement
by one.
