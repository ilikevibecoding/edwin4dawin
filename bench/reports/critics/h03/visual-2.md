## (b) Mean and gate

- Mean of the 28 scored medians: **5.12**.
- Gate: **FAIL**. Mean 5.12 < 9.0; all 28 scored categories are below 8.0; all 13 visible critical categories are below 9.25 (best critical median is 7.0 for lighting and silhouette).
- Hard failures reported: propeller absent at rest (aircraft_front B4-C5); uniform roughness across the aircraft (aircraft_rear, aircraft_front) and across the cockpit interior, the container terminal and the street facades; semi-opaque white streak spray at touchdown (water_landing C7-F8, categories 12 and 13); clone palms (city_north A7/D7/E8/H8); clone houses in rows (foliage_suburb A3-H5).

## (c) The 15 worst remaining defects, ranked and grouped by owner

Ranking is weighted toward the user's priorities (highway, foliage, roofs/skyline from the air, surface textures, float touchdown).

### Highway
1. **Arterial with no furniture at any junction** — highway_200m D2-H8. A flat grey ribbon with dashes across khaki lots: no median, turn pockets, signal heads, signage, lamp posts, shoulder lines, drainage or mown-verge strip; side-street junctions have no markings or kerb radii. Fix: a junction kit (signal mast arms, stop bars, turn-pocket striping) instanced at every arterial/side-street crossing, a 2 m mown-verge strip in a different green than lots, lamp poles every 40 m, a raised median on 4-lane sections.
2. **Causeway deck is a clean flat extrusion** — highway_bridge E4-H8 (also city_200m G6-H6). One lamp post (F4) on 400 m, no expansion-joint lines, no delineators/reflectors on barriers, no sign gantry before the pylon, no wheel-path wear, parapet without a top rail or scuppers, pylon without an aviation light. Fix: joint decals every 30 m, delineator instancing on the barrier tops, a gantry 150 m before the pylon, a wear mask in the wheel paths, pole row at 40-50 m spacing, red pylon light.
3. **Bridges and causeways at range are bare towers and lines** — aircraft_front C2-C3, harbor A2-C3, sunset D3-E4 (no visible cables on cable-stay towers), glass A2-H2 (deck without parapets/poles), highway_aerial C4-D4 (road lying on the canopy with no verges or embankment), cloudy A4 (causeway lands on the island without an abutment). Fix: cable fans as alpha-tested line strips in the mid LOD, parapet and pole-row strips visible to 1 km, an embankment/abutment mesh at every land transition, shoulder/verge strips along on-island sections.

### Vegetation
4. **Crown sprites and trunkless canopy edges** — foliage_park C5-D6 (flat jagged cut-outs), foliage_suburb E5-F5 and D6 (cut-out edges, no trunks under the grove edge), shore_beach F2-H3 and G3 (canopy meets sand as a hard line with no trunks). Fix: crown meshes with proper normals at LOD1 instead of camera-facing cards, trunk geometry kept in LOD0/1 at canopy edges, a scrub/understory scatter 3-8 m wide on every canopy boundary.
5. **Canopy from the air is a single-species mat of same-size lobes** — highway_aerial B4-E7 (single green, no emergents), cloudy A6-D7, skyline_high A6-B7, city_500m B2-E2; far shores are hedge bands (aircraft_rear A3-H3, water_landing A3-B3); foliage_park B6-C7 has drifted to a dry yellow-olive tint. Fix: 3-4 crown radii per species with 10-15 % emergents (royal palm, mahogany), per-species albedo (sea grape, gumbo limbo, palm), lighter species mixed into shore bands, pull the olive tint back toward tropical hardwood green.
6. **Clone palms and evenly scattered lot trees** — city_north A7/D7/E8/H8 (identical fronds and shadow blobs, hard failure), highway_200m A4-B6 and foliage_park E6/G6 (single spheres at even spacing). Fix: 3+ palm variants with rotation/scale/lean jitter, clustered placement along lot edges and driveways rather than uniform scatter, street-tree rows on avenues.

### Facades + roofs
7. **Blank pale slab roofs at 130-500 m (user priority 3)** — city_500m A5/C6-D6/F7/G7-H7, city_200m D5-E6/C5/D6/D7 and the flat disc crown at B3, city_north A6-B6, island_pass A2-D2, highway_200m E2-F3/F4-G4/G6-H7, foliage_suburb and foliage_park every box, harbor A5/C4/D3-E4 warehouses. Fix: a roof material distinct from the sidewalk (dark membrane/gravel, albedo 0.25-0.35) with a parapet lip that casts a shadow line, an instanced rooftop kit of 3-8 units per roof (HVAC, stair head, tank, skylight rows) scaled to footprint, pitched shingle/tile roofs with ridges and AC units on houses, pool decks and mechanical penthouses on beachfront towers.
8. **Skyline crowns bare, modules copy-pasted, ground floors dead** — skyline_high C3-E4 and island_pass A2-E3 (flat-topped extrusions at range), city_500m D4/B5 (same facade module twice), city_200m C5/D6/D7 and city_north D6-G6 (one window module on every low-rise, no entrances), street_2m C4-D4 (storefronts as red/blue bars on flat planes, glass without reflectance A1-B4). Fix: crown/mast/mechanical-floor kit applied in the far LOD, module shuffling with podium massing on 30 % of towers, a modelled ground-floor kit (doors, awnings, columns, canopies) and reflective glass at street level.

### Streets + roads
9. **Uniform surfaces and missing markings (user priority 4)** — street_2m A5-H8 (one-tone asphalt, no stop line, crosswalk, manholes, gutters, patches or sidewalk joints at 2 m), city_500m F5-H6 and city_200m A5-B8 (blank plazas), city_north A8-H8 (blank grey lot), harbor A5-D8 (near-white terminal slab with no yard grid, tyre marks or oil stains). Fix: an asphalt detail set (wheel-path wear, patch and crack decals, manhole/drain instancing), stop bars and crosswalks at every intersection, a sidewalk joint grid with kerb stones, plaza paving patterns and parking-bay striping, terminal yard grid decals and a concrete albedo of 0.35-0.45.
10. **Lighting infrastructure absent where the camera is at 200-500 m** — city_north A7-H8 (six-lane avenue, no poles or signals), city_200m C7-E7 and G6-H6, city_500m A6-H8, harbor A3-G5 (no high-mast lights on a 1 km terminal), night A2-H3 (no lamp lines or headlights). Fix: pole instancing that survives to 600 m, signal heads at downtown intersections, 30 m high-mast towers every 60-80 m on the terminal, lamp rows and headlight streams at night.

### Terrain + shore
11. **Procedural tells on the coast** — cloudy C4-D4 and highway_aerial D4-D5 (a perfectly elliptical lagoon), shore_beach F2-H3 and island_pass B3 (hard sand-to-canopy/street lines), highway_aerial A3-H4 (identical rim width on every islet), cloudy A5-A6 (bare sand blotches), harbor A6-D8 (quay wall without fenders, bollards or tide stain), island_pass A4-D3 and shore_beach G4-H4 (no or faint shorebreak foam). Fix: noise the lagoon outline and vary rim width per islet, add a scrub/wrack transition band and a tide-stain/fender kit on quays, a depth-and-swell driven foam edge with variable density.

### Water rendering
12. **Missing local reflections, truncated sun path, glint speckle** — aircraft_rear C6-F8, glass F6-H8, water_landing C6-F7 (no reflection of the aircraft on calm water, only its shadow), highway_bridge E6-F8 and city_200m G5-H6 (no pier/tower reflections), sunset E2-F4 (the sun path ends before the bridge and the foreground A5-H8 has no glitter — a regression from h00), shore_beach E4-H5 (uniform fine speckle instead of a directional path), every frame (one ripple frequency). Fix: planar or SSR reflection for near-field objects, restore a microfacet glitter term that widens toward the camera, filtered specular normals (LEAN/Toksvig) at distance, a multi-band spectrum (swell 30-60 m + wind sea + capillary) with wind direction.

### Water physics
13. **Float touchdown and planing (user priority 5)** — water_landing C6-F8 and water_step C6-F8: zero displaced water (no bow wave off the float noses, no hollow behind the step, no V-wake or stern waves), spray drawn as thin semi-opaque white streamer fans that read as air-flow lines (hard failure on 12/13), the aircraft level with float sterns buried instead of nose-up on the step (C5-F7); at rest and taxiing the floats show no wet band, meniscus or wake (aircraft_rear C5-F7, aircraft_front C6-F6, glass C6-H8); the one boat wake (highway_aerial E8) has no Kelvin structure. Fix: a per-float displacement stamp (hump ahead, hollow behind, Kelvin V with spreading transverse waves) driven by speed and immersion, thin curved translucent chine spray sheets plus an impact splash at contact, a foam patch decal with 5-10 s life and bubble texture, pitch-up 4-6 deg on the step with heave/roll from four-point buoyancy, a waterline darkening band on the float hulls, Kelvin arms and 30-60 s foam decay on boats.

### Clouds + lighting
14. **Shadows that do not behave like shadows** — aircraft_rear A6-H8 and highway_bridge A4-D7 (shadows on water are uniformly black, hard-edged and show none of the piers; the sky-reflection term should survive inside a shadow), skyline_high A2-H8 and cloudy A3-H8 (a full cumulus deck casts no shadow pattern on city or water), skyline_high overall (palette gone grey-blue under the deck: water muted teal at B5-H6, suburbs grey), night B5/G5 (nav-light halos are ~40 px round sprites with hard edges). Fix: sun-only shadow term on water with pier casters, a projected cloud-coverage shadow map on terrain and water, keep sun colour/saturation when the deck is broken, physically sized glare with soft falloff.

### Aircraft
15. **Hero-asset gaps** — aircraft_front B4-C5 (no propeller blades at rest, hard failure; cowl a blank black cylinder with a chrome cone), glass C4-E5 (no cabin door seam, handle or hinges on a cabin aircraft), aircraft_rear/aircraft_front C4-F6 (uniform roughness across paint, struts and floats, hard failure; no exhaust, chipping or float-step scuffing), cockpit C7-F8 and D5-H7 (fingerless mitten hands on a tube yoke, flat decal dials without bezels or glass, windshield with no glass). Fix: crisp blade geometry below mid RPM, cowl inlets/exhaust/cowl-flap seams, door cut lines with handles, roughness/detail maps separating satin paint, matte anti-slip float decks and oxidised aluminium, rigged hands, 3D bezels with glass, Fresnel on all glazing.

## (d) Regressions h00 -> h03 (with evidence)

1. **Overall palette desaturated under the new cloud deck (protected: overall colour palette, tropical water colour)** — skyline_high: same camera, h00 shows a deep blue sky, turquoise water in the channel B5-D8 and warm suburbs; h03 shows a grey-blue sky, muted teal water at B5-H6 and grey suburbs. The new deck (clouds4) is an improvement in volume, but the dome-following water base (waterrender) plus the deck removes the tropical read from the only 1.2 km frame. Category 9 in this frame: 6.
2. **Sunset sun path truncated (category 26)** — sunset: h00 has a glitter path from the sun to the frame bottom (E2-H8) that widens toward the camera; h03 has a broad diffuse band that ends before the bridge at E2-F4 and a foreground A5-H8 with no glitter fragments. Physically the path should extend and widen toward the viewer. Score 26 dropped to 5 in this frame.
3. **Water colour at grazing angles moved from cyan to deep sky-blue/navy (protected: tropical water colour; flag, not condemn)** — glass G5-H8 and aircraft_rear A7-D8: h00 bright cyan, h03 deep blue with turquoise only in the shallows. Physically defensible for a sideways camera reflecting the sky, but it is a visible change to a protected strength and needs the user's sign-off. Every top-down frame (island_pass, shore_beach, highway_aerial, city_200m, cloudy) keeps the turquoise.
4. **Foliage tint drift to dry yellow-olive** — foliage_park B6-C7 and foliage_suburb D5-G7: the darker, more varied canopy is a net improvement, but the olive/yellow tones read as dry scrub rather than tropical hardwood and the darker mass makes the flat cut-out crown edges at foliage_park C5-D6 more visible than in h00.
5. **Touchdown spray now reads more like "lines of white"** — water_landing C7-F8: h00's giant opaque feather fans (a hard failure) were shrunk to thin streamer fans; the opacity problem is smaller but the result is closer to the user's complaint ("air flow around it, lines of white") because nothing else (displacement, sheets, foam patch) was added.

## (e) The single most valuable fix for the whole world right now

**Give roofs and ground planes their own materials and a rooftop kit.** From every altitude the player actually flies at (130-500 m: city_500m, city_200m, city_north, island_pass, highway_200m, foliage_suburb, foliage_park, harbor, skyline_high) the frame is dominated by pale slabs that are the same material as the sidewalks and plazas; the new gridded facades and street markings are undercut because the largest visible surfaces in the frame are blank. A dark membrane/gravel roof material with a parapet lip shadow, an instanced kit of 3-8 rooftop units scaled to footprint, pitched roofs with vents on houses, and plaza/lot paving separated from sidewalks would raise categories 20, 19, 18, 17 and 29 in ten frames at once and directly answers the user's "roofs read as pale blank slabs from 200-500 m". The highway furniture pass (defects 1-3) is the next most valuable and is the user's first priority, but it changes one ribbon per frame while roofs change the whole frame.
