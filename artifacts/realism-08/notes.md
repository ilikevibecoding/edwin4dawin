# Realism pass 08 — knight + ogre volume

- Knight: diagonal key-light gradient on the torso with a leading-edge rim
  stroke; domed shield with radial light and lit boss; helmet becomes a graded
  steel dome with a hot specular; sword blade graded with an edge gleam near
  the tip; soft under-face shadow.
- Ogre: pear torso clipped and graded (lit upper-left -> shaded lower-right)
  with a rim-light stroke on the leading edge; belly, head and club all pick
  up gradients; brow highlight added.

Verified in unit crops: both melee troops read as lit volumes instead of
stickered flats.
