# Realism pass 27 — cliff-terrain border

- The stone-slab walkway is gone. The outer band is now packed earth: broad
  tonal patches, 64 pebbles/root flecks, and a vertical light gradient.
- Grass creeps from the field edge onto the earth (56 seeded tufts with lit
  caps) so the plateau blends into the landscape organically.
- Six grass-topped cliff outcrops (irregular dirt mounds with strata lines,
  turf caps with lit crests, contact shadows) punctuate the band, echoing the
  terrain vocabulary of the reference in fully original geometry.
- Corner boulders, trees, rocks and shrubs remain on top of the new ground.

Verified in the band crops: the arena now sits in a landscape rather than on
a tiled patio.
